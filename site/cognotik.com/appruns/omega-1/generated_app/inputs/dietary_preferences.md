# Dietary Preferences — Maya Chen

## Dietary Restrictions

- **Pescatarian**: No meat (beef, pork, poultry). Fish and seafood are welcome and preferred.
- **No raw onion**: Cooked onion is completely fine. Raw onion in salads or salsas causes digestive distress.
- **Tree nut allergy (moderate)**: Avoid almonds, cashews, walnuts in all forms. Pine nuts, pumpkin seeds, sunflower seeds, and sesame are all fine. Coconut is fine.

## Allergies and Intolerances

| Allergen     | Severity | Notes                                      |
|--------------|----------|--------------------------------------------|
| Tree nuts    | Moderate | Almonds, cashews, walnuts — avoid entirely |
| Raw onion    | Mild     | Cooked is fine; raw causes bloating        |

## Food Preferences

### Favorite Flavors
- **Loves**: Umami, smoky, mildly spicy, citrusy brightness, deep savory
- **Enjoys**: Bitter greens (arugula, radicchio), fermented flavors (miso, kimchi, pickles)
- **Tolerates**: Sweetness in context (e.g., mango salsa, teriyaki glaze) but dislikes overly sweet meals
- **Dislikes**: Bland, mushy, or overly creamy dishes. "Food should have opinions."

### Preferred Cooking Methods
- Roasting (especially vegetables — she loves caramelization)
- Searing and pan-frying fish
- Steaming rice and grains
- Raw preparations for fish (sashimi, ceviche) — she is comfortable with this
- Slow-simmered soups and stews on weekends when she has time

### Texture Preferences
- Loves contrast: crispy + tender, creamy + crunchy
- Dislikes: uniformly soft or mushy textures (overcooked pasta, soggy vegetables)
- Appreciates: al dente grains, crispy skin on fish, toasted seeds as toppings

## Meal Preferences

### Meal Frequency
- **3 meals per day** (breakfast, lunch, dinner)
- **1 afternoon snack** (around 3:30 PM — her creative energy dip)
- No late-night eating (she is working on this habit)

### Meal Timing
- **Breakfast**: 8:30 AM (she needs a gentle alarm and a reason to get up)
- **Lunch**: 1:00 PM
- **Snack**: 3:30 PM
- **Dinner**: 7:00 PM

### Meal Size Preferences
- **Breakfast**: Light to moderate — something that feels like a treat, not a chore
- **Lunch**: Moderate and satisfying — her main productive meal
- **Snack**: Small, no-cook or minimal prep
- **Dinner**: Moderate to hearty — her wind-down ritual; she enjoys cooking dinner as a creative break

## Budget and Logistics

### Budget
- **Weekly budget**: $90 (including birthday dinner splurge)
- **Standard per-meal budget**: ~$8–12
- **Birthday dinner budget**: Up to $25 for ingredients (Day 4 of the plan)
- Prefers quality over quantity — would rather have one excellent piece of salmon than a pile of mediocre protein

### Shopping Preferences
- Shops at a mix of: local Asian grocery (preferred for fish, miso, tofu, produce), farmers market (Saturday mornings — she loves this ritual), and a standard grocery store for staples
- Strongly prefers **seasonal produce**
- Buys **organic** for the "dirty dozen" items when budget allows
- Loves discovering one new ingredient per week — open to suggestions

### Cooking Capacity
- **Time available**: ~6–8 hours total per week for cooking
- **Weekday cooking**: 20–35 minutes max (she gets absorbed in work)
- **Weekend cooking**: Up to 90 minutes — enjoys it as a meditative activity
- **Equipment**: Well-stocked kitchen. Has: cast iron skillet, rice cooker, immersion blender, good chef's knife, sheet pans, Dutch oven. No air fryer, no stand mixer.
- **Meal prep preference**: Light Sunday prep (cook grains, wash greens, make one sauce or dressing) + daily cooking for freshness

## Health and Wellness Goals

### Primary Health Goals
1. **Sustained energy**: Avoid the 2 PM crash that derails her afternoon illustration sessions
2. **Hormone balance**: Irregular cycles; interested in seed cycling and phytoestrogen-rich foods
3. **Digestive health**: Sensitive gut; benefits from fermented foods, fiber, and avoiding raw alliums
4. **Mental clarity**: Needs sharp focus for detailed illustration work — interested in omega-3s, B vitamins, and adaptogenic herbs

### Nutritional Priorities
- **Omega-3 rich foods**: Salmon, sardines, mackerel, chia seeds, flaxseed
- **Iron**: Important for her (pescatarian, tends toward low iron) — lentils, dark leafy greens, pumpkin seeds
- **B12**: Eggs, fish, nutritional yeast
- **Magnesium**: Dark chocolate (yes, this is a priority), leafy greens, pumpkin seeds
- **Phytoestrogens**: Flaxseed, edamame, tempeh (for hormone balance)
- Balanced macronutrients — not tracking strictly, but wants to feel satisfied
