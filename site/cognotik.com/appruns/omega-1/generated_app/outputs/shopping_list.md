## STORAGE & SHELF LIFE GUIDE

- Mushrooms (store in paper bag)

## NOTES FOR SHOPPING

### Organic Priorities (Dirty Dozen)
When budget allows, prioritize organic for:
- Spinach & leafy greens
- Bell peppers
- Mushrooms
- Carrots
- Garlic

### Quality Considerations
- **Fish:** Prioritize wild-caught over farmed (higher omega-3s)
- **Eggs:** Choose pasture-raised when possible
- **Oils:** Extra virgin olive oil & sesame oil for best flavor
- **Miso:** Choose unpasteurized for maximum probiotics
- **Honey:** Raw honey preferred for enzyme content

### Seasonal Adjustments
- **Spring:** Asparagus, fresh herbs, tender greens
- **Summer:** Bell peppers, zucchini, berries
- **Fall:** Root vegetables, mushrooms, squash
- **Winter:** Citrus, hearty greens, stored root vegetables

### Budget-Saving Tips
1. Buy pantry staples in bulk (oils, spices, seeds)
2. Purchase fish on sale and freeze
3. Use farmers market for seasonal produce (cheaper & fresher)
4. Make fermented vegetables at home (cheaper than store-bought)
5. Buy canned fish (sardines, tuna) for backup meals
