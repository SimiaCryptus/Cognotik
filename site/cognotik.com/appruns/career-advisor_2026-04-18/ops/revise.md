**Synthesize the following inputs:**
- The person's profile, resume, and stated goals
- The initial brainstormed ideas
- All clarifying questions and their answers (if provided)
- The professional materials review findings
- Any prior rounds' recommendations

**Produce a structured recommendations document with these sections:**

---
