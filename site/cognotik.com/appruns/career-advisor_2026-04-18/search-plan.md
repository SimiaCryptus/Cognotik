# Job Search Strategy: Alex Chen

This document outlines the tactical search parameters and strategies for identifying Alex Chen's next career move. These instructions are intended for a search agent to filter and prioritize opportunities that align with the "Staff-level Multiplier" career phase.

## General Instructions

*   **Primary Target Role:** Staff Software Engineer, Principal Engineer, or Technical Lead (IC-track).
*   **Secondary Target Role:** Engineering Manager (only if the role is at a Series B/C startup and allows for 40% technical contribution/architectural oversight).
*   **Compensation Floor:** $250,000 Total Compensation (TC). Prioritize roles with a base salary of $200k+ and liquid or high-probability equity (Series D+ or Public).
*   **Core Tech Stack:** Go (Golang), Kubernetes (K8s), Python, AWS, Distributed Systems.
*   **Domain Focus:** Fintech, Developer Tools, Platform Engineering, or AI/ML Infrastructure.
*   **Location:** Seattle, WA (Hybrid/On-site) or Remote (specifically for SF/NYC-based companies offering Bay Area compensation).
*   **Company Stage:** Prioritize Series C through Public. Avoid early-stage (Seed/A) unless the founding team is elite and the equity package is exceptional.
*   **The "Multiplier" Filter:** Look for job descriptions that emphasize "architectural stewardship," "leading cross-team initiatives," "authoring RFCs," or "building paved roads." Avoid roles that read like "Super-Senior" positions focused solely on feature delivery.

---

## Strategy 1: Tier-1 Fintech Infrastructure
Target the "Fintech Premium" by identifying roles at established, high-valuation financial technology firms where Alex's domain expertise in PCI-DSS and real-time payment pipelines is a direct match.

*   **Target Companies:** Stripe, Plaid, Brex, Adyen, Chime, Marqeta, Modern Treasury, Lithic.
*   **Search Keywords:** "Staff Engineer Fintech," "Infrastructure Go," "Payments Architect," "Ledger Systems Engineer."
*   **Agent Task:** Identify open Staff-level roles in "Core Payments," "Banking-as-a-Service," or "Money Movement" teams.

---

## Strategy 2: Developer Tools & Platform Engineering
Leverage Alex's Kubernetes mastery and "Paved Road" philosophy. These companies value engineers who build tools for other engineers.

*   **Target Companies:** Datadog, Snowflake, HashiCorp, Vanta, Pulumi, Temporal, Sentry.
*   **Search Keywords:** "Staff Platform Engineer," "Kubernetes Operator Developer," "Developer Experience Architect," "Distributed Systems Go."
*   **Agent Task:** Filter for roles that involve building internal platforms, CI/CD orchestration, or cloud-native infrastructure tools.

---

## Strategy 3: AI/ML Infrastructure (The "MLOps" Pivot)
Target the high-growth AI sector by positioning Alex's backend/K8s skills as the foundation for scaling ML workloads.

*   **Target Companies:** Anthropic, OpenAI, Weights & Biases, Anyscale, Pinecone, Together AI.
*   **Search Keywords:** "ML Infrastructure Engineer," "Staff Engineer Ray/Kubeflow," "AI Platform Engineer," "Scaling Inference Go."
*   **Agent Task:** Look for roles that require scaling large-scale distributed systems to support model training or inference, even if the candidate doesn't have a deep ML research background.

---

## Strategy 4: High-Growth Series C/D "Hidden Market"
Identify companies that have recently raised significant funding and are scaling their engineering org from 50 to 150+ people. These companies are desperate for "adults in the room."

*   **Target Criteria:** Companies listed on "Breakout List" or "Wealthfront Career-Launching Companies" within the Fintech/SaaS space.
*   **Agent Task:** Find the VP of Engineering or Head of Infrastructure for these companies. The goal is to facilitate "skip-level" networking rather than applying through portals.
*   **Priority Verticals:** Climate Tech (e.g., Watershed), Cybersecurity (e.g., Wiz), and Vertical SaaS.

---

## Strategy 5: Compensation & Liquidity Filtering
Ensure the $250K+ goal is realistic by filtering for companies with clear paths to liquidity.

*   **Agent Task:** For any private company identified, verify their last valuation and funding round. Prioritize those with active secondary markets (e.g., via EquityZen or CartaX) or those nearing IPO.
*   **Red Flags:** Companies offering high equity but low base ($160k or less) or companies with "lifestyle" growth rates that cannot support $250k+ TC.

---

## Strategy 6: Seattle Hub Optimization
Focus on the local Seattle market for companies that value UW alumni and local presence.

*   **Target Companies:** Amazon (L7/Principal), Google (L6), Microsoft (L64+), and Seattle-based unicorns like Remitly or Highspot.
*   **Agent Task:** Identify roles in Seattle-based "Cloud" or "Platform" divisions where Alex's AWS and K8s certifications provide high signal.
