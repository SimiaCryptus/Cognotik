# Comparative Analysis ## Actual: Friday Launch - **Outcome:** Crash. High weekend traffic + reduced staff availability. ## Counterfactual 1: Tuesday Launch - **Analysis:** Traffic might have been lower or ramped up slower. Staff would be fully available to patch issues immediately. - **Likely Outcome:** Issues detected early, hotfix deployed, crash avoided. ## Counterfactual 2: Auto-scaling Enabled - **Analysis:** Infrastructure would adapt to load spikes regardless of day. - **Likely Outcome:** No crash, but higher cloud costs. ## Insight The root cause was lack of scalability, exacerbated by timing. Auto-scaling is the more robust preventive measure.
