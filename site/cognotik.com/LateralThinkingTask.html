# Lateral Thinking Results ## Technique: Reversal **Provocation:** Make patients wait *longer*. **Idea:** If waiting is inevitable, make it productive. Perform triage and initial testing *during* the wait or transport. **Breakthrough:** Shift medical process to the waiting period. ## Technique: Random Stimulus **Stimulus:** "Hotel" **Idea:** Treat patients like guests. Give them pagers/apps so they can wait elsewhere comfortably. **Breakthrough:** Decouple "waiting" from "waiting room". ## Recommended Approach Implement a "Mobile Triage" system where initial diagnostics happen before arrival or in the lobby, and allow patients to wait remotely for non-critical issues.
