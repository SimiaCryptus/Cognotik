Adaptive Planning Mode

+

+ The Strategist. An iterative, self-correcting cognitive engine that thinks, acts, and reflects to solve ambiguous or evolving problems. +

+
+ +
+

How It Works

+
+
+ +

Iterative Loop

+

Operates in cycles of "Think, Act, Reflect", allowing it to adjust its plan based on real-time feedback and task results.

+
+
+ +

Dynamic State

+

Maintains a structured internal state (Goals, Knowledge, Context) that evolves as it gathers information.

+
+
+ +

Cognitive Strategies

+

Can adopt different personas or "strategies" (e.g., Scientist, Agile Developer) to suit the nature of the task.

+
+
+
+ +
+

Cognitive Strategies

+

+ Adaptive Planning isn't one-size-fits-all. It uses specialized schemas to structure its reasoning. +

+
+
+
Project Manager
+

The Standard. Focuses on goals, facts, and next steps. Ideal for general-purpose task execution and planning.

+
+
+
Scientific Researcher
+

The Investigator. Uses the scientific method: formulating hypotheses, gathering evidence, and proving or refuting theories.

+
+
+
Agile Developer
+

The Coder. Follows TDD cycles (Red/Green/Refactor). Manages user stories, acceptance criteria, and a backlog of tasks.

+
+
+
Critical Auditor
+

The Security Expert. Focuses on risk assessment, compliance checklists, and vulnerability scanning. It tries to break things.

+
+
+
Creative Writer
+

The Storyteller. Manages narrative structure, themes, character arcs, and tone. Iteratively drafts and refines content.

+
