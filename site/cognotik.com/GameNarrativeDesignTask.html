# Narrative Design: The Last Signal ## Premise A lone operator at a deep-space listening post receives a transmission that predates humanity. ## Branching Point: The Decryption **Context:** The signal is encrypted with a hazardous memetic virus. - **Choice A:** Isolate the signal to protect the network. (Safety) - **Choice B:** Interface directly to decode it faster. (Risk) ## Consequences - **If A:** The signal degrades. You gain partial info but stay sane. - **If B:** You learn the truth about the "Precursors" but begin hallucinating. ## Endings 1. **The Guardian:** You destroy the signal to save Earth. 2. **The Prophet:** You merge with the signal and ascend.
