# Economy Design: Galactic Trader ## Resources 1. **Credits (Soft Currency):** Earned by trading, missions. Used for ship repairs, fuel. 2. **Dark Matter (Premium):** Purchased or rare drops. Used for cosmetics, convenience. 3. **Scrap (Material):** Salvaged from battles. Used for crafting. ## Sinks & Faucets - **Faucet:** Daily missions (Credits), Mining (Scrap). - **Sink:** Market taxes, Ship insurance, Crafting failure risk. ## Monetization Strategy - **Battle Pass:** "Smuggler's Run" - 30 tiers of cosmetic ship skins. - **Convenience:** Inventory expansion slots. - **No Pay-to-Win:** Premium currency cannot buy endgame weapons directly.
