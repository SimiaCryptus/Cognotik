# Economy Design: Galactic Trader ## Resource System 1. **Credits (Currency):** Primary soft currency. Source: Trading, Missions. Sink: Repairs, Fuel, Market Tax. 2. **Dark Matter (Premium):** Hard currency. Source: IAP, Rare Achievements. Sink: Cosmetics, Convenience. 3. **Scrap (Material):** Crafting resource. Source: Salvage, Mining. Sink: Ship Upgrades. ## Progression System - **Tiers:** 50 Levels - **Style:** Branching (Trader vs. Pirate paths) - **Skill Tree:** 3 Branches (Navigation, Combat, Mercantile) - **Time to Max:** ~120 hours for average player ## Loot & Rewards - **Common:** Scrap, Fuel Cells (Drop Rate: 40%) - **Rare:** Ship Blueprints, High-grade components (Drop Rate: 5%) - **Bosses:** Guaranteed Epic component + Title ## Monetization Strategy (Free-to-Play) - **Battle Pass:** "Smuggler's Run" (30 tiers). Free: Resources. Premium: Exclusive "Void" Ship Skin. - **Convenience:** Cargo Hold Expansion, Warp Drive Cooldown reduction. - **Risk Assessment:** Low Pay-to-Win risk; premium items are cosmetic or time-saving only. ## Engagement Hooks - **Daily:** "Trade Route of the Day" (Bonus Credits). - **Seasonal:** 3-month seasons with unique galaxy sectors opening up. - **Retention:** Login streak grants increasing amounts of Dark Matter. ## Economy Forecast (6 Months) - **Month 1:** Healthy. High credit velocity as players upgrade starter ships. - **Month 3:** Potential Inflation. Credit accumulation may outpace sinks. - **Recommendation:** Introduce "Guild Tax" or high-cost capital ships in Month 3 update.
