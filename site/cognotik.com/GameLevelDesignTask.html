# Level Design: The Forgotten Catacombs ## Overview **Theme:** Ancient underground ruins **Difficulty:** Medium **Est. Duration:** 15-20 mins ## Layout [Start] -> [Hall of Echoes] -> [Puzzle Chamber] -> [Boss Arena] | [Hidden Armory] ## Zones 1. **Hall of Echoes**: A long, narrow corridor filled with skeletal warriors. - *Encounter:* 3 Skeleton Warriors, 1 Archer. 2. **Puzzle Chamber**: A room with pressure plates and locked doors. - *Mechanic:* Sequence puzzle to unlock the path forward. 3. **Hidden Armory** (Secret): Accessible via a cracked wall in the Hall. - *Reward:* Rare weapon chest. ## Pacing - Start: Low intensity (exploration) - Mid: Medium intensity (combat & puzzle) - Climax: High intensity (Boss fight)
