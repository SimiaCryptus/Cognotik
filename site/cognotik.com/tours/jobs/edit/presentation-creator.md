# Presentation Creator — Demo Walkthrough

A step-by-step guide to creating and iteratively updating professional presentations using the Cognitik AI-powered
Presentation Creator.

---

## 00:00 — Introduction

Welcome to the Presentation Creator demo. This AI-powered tool lets you create and update professional presentations
from simple notes. Let's walk through the full workflow.

---

## 00:08 — Logging In

First, let's log in with our credentials. After entering the login details we are authenticated and can see the
**Cognitik App Hub** — the central place where all available applications are listed.

---

## 00:54 — Launching the Presentation Creator

From the App Hub, let's launch the **Presentation Creator**.

Here's the Presentation Creator interface:

- **Left panel** — the editor, with tabs for *Notes* and *Update Notes*
- **Right panel** — the preview panel where the generated presentation will appear

---

## 01:24 — Configuring AI Models

Before we start, let's configure our AI models in the **Settings** tab:

- **Smart model** — selected for complex tasks such as initial slide generation
- **Fast model** — selected for quick iterative edits

With models configured, let's switch back to the **Notes** tab.

---

## 01:42 — Entering Presentation Notes

We'll enter our presentation notes. These can be bullet points, headings, or any structured text describing the content
we want in our slides.

Our notes describe a **Quarterly Business Review** covering:

- Key metrics
- Highlights
- Challenges
- Next steps

---

## 02:31 — Generating the Presentation

With our notes ready, let's click **Draft Presentation**. The AI analyzes the notes and generates a complete slide deck.
We can monitor generation progress through the session link.

*(AI generation in progress — time-lapsed)*

---

## 03:38 — Reviewing the Generated Slides

Excellent! The presentation has been generated. Let's review it in the preview panel.

The AI has created professional slides from our notes, complete with proper formatting and layout — ready to use with
minimal adjustment.

---

## 04:02 — Making Updates

Now let's make some changes. We'll switch to the **Update Notes** tab to describe the modifications we want in natural
language:

- A **color scheme change**
- An **additional slide** about a new partnership
- Various **content updates**

---

## 04:47 — Applying the Updates

With our update notes ready, we click **Update Presentation**. The AI applies the requested changes to the existing
presentation.

*(AI applying changes — time-lapsed)*

---

## 05:20 — Reviewing the Updated Presentation

The presentation has been updated. Notice:

- The **new color scheme** applied throughout
- The **new partnership slide** added to the deck

We can continue iterating with further update notes as needed — the workflow is fully incremental.

---

## 05:45 — Downloading the Presentation

We can also **download the presentation as an HTML file** using the download button in the preview toolbar, making it
easy to share or host the finished deck.

---

## 05:55 — Summary

That's the complete workflow of the **Presentation Creator**:

1. Enter notes describing your desired content
2. Click *Draft Presentation* — AI generates a full slide deck
3. Describe changes in *Update Notes* — AI applies them iteratively
4. Download the finished presentation as HTML

From simple notes to a polished, professional presentation with easy iterative updates. Thank you for watching.
