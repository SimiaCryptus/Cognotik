# AI Job Hunter — Automated Job Search Pipeline

> **Source video:** `source/job-hunter.mp4`
> **Edited video:** `edit/job-hunter.mp4`

---

## Overview

This video walks through the complete **AI Job Hunter** workflow — an automated job search pipeline that uses AI to research and apply for jobs matching your profile.

---

## Sections

### 00:00:05 — Introduction

Welcome to AI Job Hunter, an automated job search pipeline that uses AI to define, research, and apply for jobs matching your profile. This walkthrough covers the complete workflow from configuration through to organized job matches and tailored applications.

---

### 00:00:23 — Logging In

Logging in to access the platform. Once authenticated, the **Cognotik App Hub** is displayed, from which any available application can be launched. We open the **AI Job Hunter**.

---

### 00:01:12 — The AI Job Hunter Interface

The AI Job Hunter interface has three main sections:

- **Configuration** — AI models, resume, and job requirements
- **Search Pipeline** — multi-step automated job search
- **Results** — matched jobs, generated applications, and logs

---

### 00:01:26 — Configuring AI Models

Selecting the AI models that will power the search:

- **Smart model** — for analysis and summarization
- **Fast model** — for high-volume processing
- **Image model** (optional) — for visual content

Configuration is saved and the models are ready.

---

### 00:01:56 — Reviewing the Resume

The resume is stored in **JSON format**, enabling the AI to precisely match skills and experience to job requirements. It contains work history, skills, education, and other relevant details.

---

### 00:02:26 — Setting Job Requirements

Defining what to look for:

- Remote preference
- Salary range
- Tech stack
- Company culture

These criteria guide the AI's search and filtering process.

---

### 00:02:53 — Exclusion List

Companies to avoid — such as those already applied to or not of interest — are specified in an exclusion list. These companies are filtered out of all search results.

---

### 00:03:15 — Running the Job Search Pipeline

The multi-step pipeline searches the web, extracts matches, and organizes them for review.

**Step 1 — Web Research**

The AI crawls the web using Google Search to find job postings matching the profile. It can search up to **500 pages** at a depth of **3 levels**.

> ⏩ *Web research in progress — time-lapsed (~17 minutes compressed to ~10 seconds)*

Web research is complete. The AI has found and analyzed potential job matches.

---

### 00:06:55 *(edited)* — Step 2: Copy Matches

Job matches are extracted and organized — each opportunity placed into its own folder for easy review. Each match now has its own file with full job details.

---

### 00:07:15 *(edited)* — Reviewing Results

The **Job Matches** tab shows all positions found during the search. Clicking on a match reveals:

- Full job description
- Company information
- How the position matches the candidate's profile

From here, the company can be researched further or a tailored application generated.

---

### 00:07:45 *(edited)* — Applications Tab

The **Applications** tab shows generated cover letters and application materials, each tailored to the specific position and company.

---

### 00:08:00 *(edited)* — Logs Tab

The **Logs** tab provides a detailed record of all pipeline activity — useful for debugging or reviewing exactly what the AI did.

---

### 00:08:10 *(edited)* — Usage and Costs

Monitoring token consumption and associated costs:

- Prompt tokens
- Completion tokens
- Total cost

This transparency helps stay within budget.

---

### 00:08:35 *(edited)* — Git Integration

AI Job Hunter includes **Git integration** for version control. Users can:

- Commit changes
- View the Git log
- Track job search history over time

This is especially useful for maintaining a record of applications sent.

---

### 00:09:00 *(edited)* — Conclusion

That's the complete AI Job Hunter workflow:

1. Configure your profile (models, resume, requirements)
2. Automated web research across hundreds of pages
3. Organized job matches with full details
4. Tailored applications powered by AI

*Thank you for watching.*
