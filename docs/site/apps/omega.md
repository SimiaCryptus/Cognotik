# Ω Omega — Build Your Own AI App, Just by Describing It

Have an idea for an AI-powered app? Just describe it in plain English, and Omega will build the whole thing for you — automatically.

## What It Does

Omega is a meta-app: an app that builds other apps. You start by writing a short description of the app you want — what input it should take, what steps it should perform, and what output it should produce. Omega then does the heavy lifting, generating:

- A clear requirements document laying out how your app will work
- A complete set of AI processing steps (the "pipeline") that power your app
- A polished, ready-to-use single-page web interface for interacting with it

No coding required — just describe your idea and watch it come to life.

## How to Use It

1. **Describe your idea.** In the Idea tab, write what your app should do — what the user provides, what processing should happen, and what results they should get. Save your idea when you're happy with it.
2. **Pick your AI models (optional).** In the Models tab, choose which AI models handle the heavy thinking, quick tasks, and any images — or just stick with the defaults.
3. **Generate your app.** In the Generate tab, run through three steps — Requirements, Pipeline, and UI — either one at a time or all at once with the "Run Entire Generation Pipeline" button. Watch live status badges as your app comes together.
4. **Refine it.** Not quite right? Head to the Iterate tab and describe what you'd like changed about the pipeline or the interface, then apply the updates.
5. **Explore and export.** Browse everything that was created in the Results tab, and use the Git tab to save versions or download your finished app as a ZIP file.

## Behind the Scenes

Omega builds your app in three simple stages:

- **Requirements** — turns your idea into a structured plan covering inputs, steps, and outputs
- **Pipeline** — creates the actual AI processing steps that make your app run
- **User Interface** — designs a complete, ready-to-use web page tailored to your app's pipeline
