# 🧠 Quiz Wiz

**Turn any idea into a playable quiz game — no coding required.**

Describe a quiz topic in a sentence or two, and Quiz Wiz's AI team designs the game, writes the questions, builds
a working web app, tests it, and hands you a link to play — all in just a few minutes.

## What It Does

You give Quiz Wiz a plain-English description of the quiz you want — the topic, who it's for, how many questions,
and any special touches you'd like. From there, the AI takes over: it designs the quiz structure, writes all the
questions and answers, builds a real playable web game, tests it to catch problems, and reviews the results. What
you get back is a ready-to-play quiz game you can open in your browser and share with anyone.

## How to Use It

1. Open Quiz Wiz and pick your AI models — the defaults work great if you're unsure.
2. Write your quiz idea in the *Quiz Idea* box (topic, audience, difficulty, number of questions, style).
3. Save your idea, then step through each card in order, clicking the button to run each step.
4. Watch the status badges move from `pending` to `done` as your quiz comes together.
5. Once the **▶️ Open Game** button lights up, click it to play your finished quiz!

Want changes? Just write your feedback in the Update box and re-run — Quiz Wiz will rebuild your game with your
notes applied, as many times as you like.

- 📝 Every question, answer, and explanation is written for you automatically
- 🎮 Get a real, polished, playable web game — not just a document
- 🧪 Your quiz is automatically tested so problems get caught before you play
- ♻️ Easily refine and tweak your quiz with simple feedback, again and again

## Behind the Scenes

Quiz Wiz works through a simple sequence of AI-powered steps:

1. **Design** — plans the quiz structure and rules
2. **Game Data** — writes all the questions and answers
3. **Build Code** — assembles the playable web app
4. **Test** — runs the game to check everything works
5. **Review** — checks for issues and flags anything to fix
6. **Update** — applies your feedback whenever you want changes
