# 🏗️ Webapp Builder
Describe the app you're dreaming of, and watch AI build it for you — no coding required.
## What It Does
Webapp Builder turns a plain-English description of an app idea into a real, working web application. You tell it
what you want — the features, the audience, the look and feel — and the AI writes all the code behind the scenes.
In minutes, you get a fully functional app you can preview right in your browser, tweak with simple follow-up
requests, and download to keep forever.
## How to Use It
1. **Describe your idea.** In the Idea tab, write a description of the app you want — what it does, who it's
for, and any features or style preferences you have in mind.
2. **Pick a build mode.** Choose Full Pipeline for careful planning on complex apps, or Simple Mode for a fast,
single-pass build.
3. **Run the pipeline.** Click Run Pipeline and watch live progress as your app comes together.
4. **Launch and explore.** Click Launch App to try it out, or preview it right inside the Results tab.
5. **Refine as needed.** Use the Update tab to describe changes or fixes — the AI revises your app on the spot.
## Why You'll Love It
- **No coding needed.** Just describe your idea in everyday language, the way you'd explain it to a friend.
- **See it instantly.** A live preview lets you try your app the moment it's built — no setup required.
- **Built-in quality checks.** An automated tester takes a screenshot, checks the console, and reviews your app
for issues, then suggests fixes you can apply with one click.
- **Yours to keep.** Download your entire project as a ZIP file, ready to host or edit anywhere.
- **Never lose progress.** Every build and update is automatically saved as a version you can revisit anytime.
- **Full transparency on cost.** See exactly how much AI usage each build costs, broken down by task.
- **Iterate freely.** Keep refining your app with plain-language feedback until it's exactly right.
## Behind the Scenes
- **Plan** — the AI thinks through your idea and sketches out the app's structure (Full Pipeline mode).
- **Build** — it writes the HTML, CSS, and JavaScript needed to bring your app to life.
- **Test** — a headless browser loads your app, capturing screenshots, console logs, and network activity.
- **Review & Update** — the AI can review test results and apply fixes or new features based on your feedback.
