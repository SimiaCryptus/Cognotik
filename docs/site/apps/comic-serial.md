# 📚 Comic Serial Generator

> Turn your ideas into an ongoing visual comic series — one episode at a time!

## What It Does

Got a story idea, an article, or just a spark of inspiration? Type or paste it in, and the Comic Serial
Generator turns it into a fully illustrated comic episode — complete with panels, artwork, and captions. Then
keep going: generate sequel after sequel, each one picking up right where the last left off, until you've built
an entire comic series from a single idea.

## How to Use It

1. **Set Your Idea** — Head to the Idea tab and write or paste your story concept, article, or narrative pitch.
   Your text saves automatically as you type.
2. **Generate Episode #1** — Go to the Pipeline tab and click **Generate Comic #1** to create the opening
   episode, complete with characters, art style, and story setup.
3. **Continue the Adventure** — Click **Generate Next Episode** whenever you want to add another chapter, or use
   **Batch Generation** to create up to 20 episodes in one go.
4. **Compile Your Comicbook** — When you're ready, click **Generate HTML Book** to combine every episode into a
   single shareable comic book you can open in any browser.

* ♾️ **A story that keeps growing** — every new episode builds naturally on the last.
* ⚡ **Build a whole series fast** — batch-generate many episodes at once instead of one at a time.

## Behind the Scenes

* 📖 **Series tab** keeps a tidy library of every episode you've created, ready to review or compile.
* 🤖 **Models tab** lets you fine-tune which AI powers your story writing, panel art, and quick processing steps.
