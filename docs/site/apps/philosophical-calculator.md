+# 🧮 Philosophical Calculator
+
+Turn messy notes into polished articles — then refract your ideas through rich analytical and creative lenses.
+
+## What It Does
+
+Philosophical Calculator takes your raw notes, transcripts, or brainstorm lists and helps you turn them into a
+structured, well-reasoned article. But it doesn't stop there — you can also explore your ideas from unexpected
+angles (like a Socratic debate or a game theory breakdown), then feed those insights back into your article with a
+single click. When you're ready to share, publish your work as a persuasive essay, a narrative story, a comic
+script, a technical tutorial, or a ready-to-launch webpage.
+
+## How to Use It
+
+1. Go to the **Input** tab, choose your AI models, and drop in your raw notes or upload files.
+2. Head to the **Pipeline** tab and run **Summarize Notes**, then **Draft Article**, to build your first draft.
+3. Open the **Analysis** tab and try a few lenses — like **Socratic Dialogue** or **Game Theory** — to stress-test
+   your ideas.
+4. Return to **Pipeline** and run **Update Article** to blend those fresh insights into your draft.
+5. Visit the **Output** tab to generate a comic script, narrative story, or a polished HTML webpage.
+
+## Why You'll Love It
+
+* **Turns messy ideas into masterpieces** — drop in unorganized notes or transcripts and watch them become
+  structured, high-impact articles.
+* **Explores your ideas from every angle** — over 10 specialized lenses, from Socratic debate to historical
+  arguments between legendary thinkers.
+* **Feeds insights back into your work** — refine and synthesize new perspectives into your core article with one
+  click.
+* **Publishes in whatever format you need** — persuasive essays, illustrated stories, comic scripts, tutorials, or
+  standalone webpages.
+* **Makes reviewing effortless** — switch between formatted previews and raw text, or expand into a distraction-free
+  fullscreen reading mode.
+
+## Behind the Scenes
+
+Philosophical Calculator moves your ideas through four simple stages:
+
+1. **Input** — Set up your AI models and add your notes, files, and any special instructions.
+2. **Pipeline** — Summarize your notes, draft your article, refine it with new insights, and add illustrations.
+3. **Analysis** — Explore your ideas through lenses like Brainstorm, Dialectical, Socratic Dialogue, Perspectives,
+   Game Theory, Historical Debate, and more.
+4. **Output** — Transform your finished article into an essay, story, comic script, tutorial, or webpage.
