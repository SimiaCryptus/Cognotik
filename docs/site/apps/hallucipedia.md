# 🪐 Hallucipedia

> The encyclopedia that doesn't exist until you look at it.

## What It Does

Hallucipedia turns a single idea — a sentence like *"A world where light travels slowly enough to be farmed"* —
into an entire, ever-expanding encyclopedia. You describe a world, and Hallucipedia builds a front page full of
tantalizing links. Click any unwritten (red) link and watch a brand-new article get written for you on the spot,
always staying consistent with everything that came before it.

## How to Use It

1. **Describe your world.** Type a sentence or two about the universe you want to explore, or pick one of the
   starter prompts.
2. **Explore the front page.** Hallucipedia generates a main portal page packed with intriguing links, some
   written, some waiting to be discovered.
3. **Click and discover.** Click a blue link to read an existing article, or click a red link to have Hallucipedia
   write it instantly, in canon with the rest of the world.
4. **Search the unwritten.** Use the search bar to look up any topic — even ones nobody has written about yet.
5. **Illustrate and refine.** Add diagrams and artwork with the Illustrate button, or hit Regenerate for a fresh
   take on any page.

## Why You'll Love It

* **A world that never runs dry** — there's always another link to click and another article to discover.
* **Consistent lore, every time** — no matter how deep you go, the facts, tone, and history never contradict
  themselves.
* **Instant visuals** — bring articles to life with diagrams, illustrations, and equations in a single click.
* **Perfect for creative work** — a playground for writers, game masters, and worldbuilders who want endless
  inspiration without the writer's block.
* **Start over anytime** — jump into a brand-new universe whenever inspiration strikes.
