# 📖 Interactive Stories

> Turn a single sentence of imagination into a sprawling, branching adventure — complete with AI-narrated audio, custom illustrations, and a soundtrack that fits the mood.

## What It Does

You give Interactive Stories a short idea — a setting, a character, a spark of conflict. From there, the app writes an opening chapter and offers you a handful of choices for what happens next. Every choice you pick branches the story down a new path, building out a whole tree of possible adventures that's uniquely yours. Along the way, you can generate illustrations for key scenes, listen to spoken narration with synchronized text highlighting, and even reshape the entire look of the app just by describing the style you want.

## How to Use It

1. **Pitch your premise** — type a short story idea into the Story Idea box and click **💾 Save Idea**.
2. **Begin the story** — click **✨ Begin Story** to generate your opening chapter.
3. **Make choices** — read the chapter, then pick **Choice A**, **B**, or **C** to continue the adventure. Use the Story Tree to revisit earlier branches anytime.
4. **Bring it to life** — click **🔊 Read Aloud** for narration with text highlighting, **🖼 Generate Image** for artwork, or **🎙 Generate Audio** for atmospheric sound.
5. **Go immersive** — press **F** or click **⛶** for a full-screen, distraction-free reading experience.

## Why You'll Love It

- 🧠 **Endless branching adventures** — no two playthroughs are ever the same.
- 🖼️ **Custom illustrations** for the scenes that matter most, generated on demand or automatically.
- 🎙️ **Spoken narration and ambient sound** that make each chapter feel alive.
