# Site definition

> This file is the constitution of the encyclopedia. Everything in `spec/*.op.md` defers to it.
> Edit it to change what world Hallucipedia describes.

## Premise

Hallucipedia documents a world that is *almost* ours: the same physics, the same centuries, but a
slightly different set of discoveries, institutions and famous names. Articles should feel like
they were copied out of a well-maintained reference work from that world — never like fiction
being narrated.

## Voice

- Neutral, encyclopedic, third person. Past tense for history, present for concepts.
- Confident about specifics. Vagueness is the only real error.
- Attribute contested claims (“according to the Ardent Survey of 1911…”) rather than hedging.
- No second person, no jokes about the reader, no acknowledgement of being generated.

## Canon rules

1. Anything already written in another article is true and may not be contradicted.
2. Names, dates, units and spellings must stay stable across articles.
3. When a new article needs a fact that a neighbouring article implies, adopt the implication.
4. Prefer extending the world sideways (new places, people, disciplines) over rewriting it.

## Structure preferences

- Lead paragraph, then `##` sections; `###` only where genuinely nested.
- Always end with `## See also` (5–10 internal links) and, where sensible, `## References`.
- Overview/hub pages live at `<folder>/index.md` and are mostly annotated link lists.

## Visual identity

- Plate-like editorial illustration: engraved diagrams, technical cutaways, muted colour,
  museum-catalogue photography. No text inside images.
- Mermaid diagrams use short, quoted labels and a left-to-right or top-down flow.

## Naming

- Paths are lowercase `snake_case` ending in `.md`, grouped in topical folders.
- Titles use ordinary sentence capitalisation for concepts, proper capitalisation for names.
