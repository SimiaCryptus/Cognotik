## Most Divisive Text

**Consensus Score:** 43.25

```
The government should implement a carbon tax to combat climate change while providing subsidies for renewable energy development.
```

**Points of Contention:**
- lack of a "just transition": progressives would want to see explicit mention of how workers in the fossil fuel industry will be supported during this shift.
- revenue neutrality vs. social investment: there would be a debate over whether the tax revenue should be returned as a dividend to citizens (to offset costs) or invested in public transit and environmental justice communities.
- environmental justice: the text doesn't specify if the renewable development will prioritize marginalized communities that have historically suffered most from pollution.
