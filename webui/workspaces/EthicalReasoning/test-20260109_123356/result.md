# Ethical Reasoning Summary

**Dilemma:** Should an autonomous vehicle prioritize the safety of its passengers over pedestrians in an unavoidable accident scenario?

**Recommendation:** The final recommendation is a hybrid "Harm-Mitigation" model that prioritizes predictable rule-adherence and vulnerability-weighted braking, mandated uniformly by law to ensure all vehicles protect the most vulnerable. The key trade-off balances the utilitarian goal of minimizing total harm against the deontological need to avoid "targeting" innocent bystanders, while navigating the paradox that overly altruistic programming might discourage the public adoption necessary to save lives.
