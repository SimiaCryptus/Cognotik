# Ethical Reasoning Summary

**Dilemma:** Should an autonomous vehicle prioritize the safety of its passengers over pedestrians in an unavoidable accident scenario?

**Recommendation:** The final recommendation is a **"Rule-Based Harm Minimization"** model that prioritizes legal predictability and the protection of vulnerable pedestrians over passenger safety. This approach navigates the central trade-off between the **utilitarian goal of minimizing total fatalities** and the **deontological requirement to avoid "targeting" innocent bystanders** to save others.
