# Ethical Reasoning Summary

**Dilemma:** Should an autonomous vehicle prioritize the safety of its passengers over pedestrians in an unavoidable accident scenario?

**Recommendation:** The final recommendation is a **Vulnerability-Weighted Harm Minimization** protocol, which prioritizes maximum braking and protects the most vulnerable road users (pedestrians) while strictly forbidding the intentional targeting of bystanders. The key trade-off involves balancing the **utilitarian goal** of minimizing total statistical deaths against the **deontological necessity** of respecting individual rights and avoiding the "targeting" of human lives as mere instruments of safety.
