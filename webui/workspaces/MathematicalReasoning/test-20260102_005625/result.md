# Solution

## Problem
Find the derivative of f(x) = x^2 * sin(x)

## Answer

$$f'(x) = (2x) \cdot \sin(x) + x^2 \cdot (\cos(x))$$

Evaluate the derivatives of the individual functions $u(x) = x^2$ and $v(x) = \sin(x)$.

## Key Steps

- Evaluate the derivatives of the individual functions $u(x) = x^2$ and $v(x) = \sin(x)$.
  - $f'(x) = (2x) \cdot \sin(x) + x^2 \cdot (\cos(x))$
