# Solution

## Problem
Solve for x: 3x + 7 = 22

## Answer

$$x = 5$$

Divide both sides of the equation by 3 to isolate $x$.

## Key Steps

- The initial equation is 3x + 7 = 22.
  - $3x + 7 = 22$
- Subtract 7 from both sides of the equation to isolate the term containing $x$.
  - $3x = 15$
- Divide both sides of the equation by 3 to isolate $x$.
  - $x = 5$
