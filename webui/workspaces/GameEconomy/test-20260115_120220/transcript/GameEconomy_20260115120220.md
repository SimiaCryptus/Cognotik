## Resource System Design

This design for **Galactic Trader** focuses on a "Logistics Strategy" model. Despite the title, the "Trading" aspect is thematic—players act as a solo blockade runner or fleet commander who must manage internal logistics to progress through a linear series of increasingly dangerous star sectors.

---

### 1. Resource Identity: Titanium Alloy (Material)
*   **Type**: Primary Construction Material.
*   **Purpose**: The physical backbone of the game. It is used for crafting ship modules, repairing hull damage, and building station infrastructure.
*   **Thematic Fit**: Represents the raw physical mass required to survive in space. It grounds the player in the "industrial" side of space strategy.

### 2. Resource Identity: Ion Cells (Currency/Energy)
*   **Type**: Operational Currency.
*   **Purpose**: Acts as both "fuel" for movement and the medium of exchange for automated services (refueling, hiring NPC escorts, or activating jump gates).
*   **Thematic Fit**: Represents the power required to keep systems running. In a world without open trading, energy is the only universal constant of value.

### 3. Resource Identity: Neural Data (Experience/Progression)
*   **Type**: Progression Resource (XP).
*   **Purpose**: Used to unlock new blueprints in the Tech Tree and "level up" the player’s command capabilities. It represents the "linear" gatekeeper of the game.
*   **Thematic Fit**: Represents the mapping of the galaxy and the reverse-engineering of alien tech.

### 4. Resource Identity: Void Shards (Premium/Catalyst)
*   **Type**: Rare Catalyst / Premium Resource.
*   **Purpose**: Used to bypass crafting timers, guarantee "Masterwork" rolls on gear, or purchase unique cosmetic ship hulls. It can also be used to "overclock" production for a limited time.
*   **Thematic Fit**: A mysterious substance found only in the hearts of collapsed stars, allowing for the manipulation of space-time.

---

### Generation Sources

 Resource | Primary Source | Amount | Frequency | Scaling |
 :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
 **Titanium Alloy** | Asteroid Mining / Salvaging Wrecks | 50–200 per node | High (Every mission) | Node richness increases in later sectors. |
 **Ion Cells** | Solar Harvesting / Mission Rewards | 10–50 per minute | Constant (Passive/Active) | Better "Solar Sails" increase passive gain. |
 **Neural Data** | Scanning Anomalies / Combat Victory | 5–20 per scan | Medium (Exploration) | Higher-tier enemies drop more complex data. |
 **Void Shards** | Boss Encounters / Daily Login / IAP | 1–5 per event | Very Low (Milestones) | Fixed; does not scale to prevent inflation. |

---

### Consumption Uses

*   **Titanium Alloy**: 
    *   *Crafting*: 500–5,000 per module.
    *   *Repairs*: 10 per 1% Hull HP.
    *   *Value*: Essential for survival; without it, the player cannot upgrade their stats to face harder sectors.
*   **Ion Cells**:
    *   *Travel*: 50 per Sector Jump.
    *   *Refining*: 100 per batch of Alloy processed.
    *   *Value*: The "Action Point" equivalent. It dictates how much the player can do in a single session.
*   **Neural Data**:
    *   *Tech Tree*: 100–10,000 per node.
    *   *Value*: The only way to unlock the next tier of equipment. It drives the linear progression.
*   **Void Shards**:
    *   *Speed-up*: 10 per hour of crafting skipped.
    *   *Quality*: 50 to ensure a "Legendary" craft.
    *   *Value*: Convenience and "Min-Maxing" for end-game players.

---

### Storage and Limits

*   **Titanium Alloy**: Limited by **Cargo Bay Capacity**. 
    *   *Rationale*: Forces players to choose between carrying raw materials or finished goods. 
    *   *Overflow*: Excess is jettisoned (lost) unless the player builds "Storage Depots."
*   **Ion Cells**: Limited by **Battery Capacity**.
    *   *Rationale*: Prevents infinite kiting/traveling. Players must return to "Sun Zones" to recharge.
    *   *Overflow*: Wasted as heat (no benefit).
*   **Neural Data**: **Unlimited**.
    *   *Rationale*: As a progression resource, capping it feels punitive and discourages exploration.
*   **Void Shards**: **Unlimited**.
    *   *Rationale*: Standard for premium resources to ensure players never lose what they purchase.

---

### Exchange Rates (Internal Conversion)
*Since Trading is disabled, these are fixed "Alchemy" or "Refinery" rates:*
*   **Refining**: 2x Raw Ore + 10 Ion Cells $\rightarrow$ 1 Titanium Alloy.
*   **Data Extraction**: 100 Titanium Alloy (Scrapped) $\rightarrow$ 5 Neural Data.
*   **Emergency Power**: 1 Void Shard $\rightarrow$ 1,000 Ion Cells (Instant refill).
*   **Market Dynamics**: There is no fluctuating market. Instead, "Sector Difficulty" acts as a tax; as players move to higher sectors, the Ion Cell cost for travel increases, requiring more efficient crafting.

---

### Sink Mechanisms & Balance

1.  **The Repair Sink (Titanium)**: As enemies get stronger, the "Titanium Tax" for repairing the ship increases. This prevents players from hoarding infinite Alloy.
2.  **The Jump Tax (Ion Cells)**: Moving between sectors consumes Ion Cells. If a player stalls in a sector too long, they may run out of energy, forcing them to "Scrap" modules to generate emergency power.
3.  **The Tech Ceiling (Neural Data)**: Each new tier of technology costs exponentially more Data. This ensures players cannot skip the linear progression of the game.
4.  **Inflation Prevention**: Because there is no player-to-player trading, inflation is impossible. The economy is a closed loop between the player and the environment. The "Sink" is the linear progression itself—old resources become obsolete as the player moves to higher-tier materials (e.g., Titanium Alloy Grade 2).

### Meaningful Choices
*   **Short-term vs. Long-term**: Do I spend my Ion Cells to mine more Titanium (Short-term survival) or use them to scan anomalies for Neural Data (Long-term progression)?
*   **Inventory Management**: My Cargo Bay is full. Do I keep the Titanium to repair my ship later, or do I scrap it for Neural Data to unlock a better shield blueprint now?
*   **Progression Paths**: Players can focus on "Industrial" upgrades (increasing storage/mining) or "Combat" upgrades (increasing data gain from kills), allowing for different playstyles within the linear path.

## Progression System Design

This progression system is designed for **Galactic Trader**, a strategy game where players manage a fleet, craft advanced components, and optimize resource chains. Despite the title, per the design constraints, "Trading" is replaced by **Contract Fulfillment** (a crafting-based delivery system) to maintain the strategy focus without a player-to-player market.

---

### 1. The Four-Resource Economy (Foundation)
To understand progression, we must define the resources being managed:
1.  **Galactic Credits (Currency):** Earned via contracts; used for facility maintenance and blueprint purchases.
2.  **Asteroid Ore (Basic Material):** Harvested from belts; the primary ingredient for early-game crafting.
3.  **Isotope Shards (Advanced Material):** Found in hazardous zones; required for high-tier tech and engine upgrades.
4.  **Data Slates (Experience/Research):** Generated through exploration and successful crafting; the primary source of XP.

---

### 2. Linear Experience Curve
The progression follows a **Linear Growth Model**, where the XP required for the next level increases by a fixed increment. This ensures that while the game gets harder, the goals remain predictable and reachable.

*   **Base XP for Level 1:** 1,000 XP
*   **Increment per Level:** +1,000 XP
*   **Formula:** $XP\ to\ Level\ (N) = N \times 1,000$
*   **Total XP to Max Level (30):** 465,000 XP

 Tier | Levels | XP per Level | Total Tier XP | Estimated Time |
 :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
 **Early Game** | 1–10 | 1k – 10k | 55,000 | 5–8 Hours |
 **Mid Game** | 11–20 | 11k – 20k | 155,000 | 20–25 Hours |
 **Late Game** | 21–30 | 21k – 30k | 255,000 | 35–45 Hours |
 **Total** | **30** | -- | **465,000** | **~60–80 Hours** |

---

### 3. Unlock Schedule & Milestones

 :--- | :--- | :--- |
 **1** | **Basics** | Starter Ship (The "Mule"), Ore Refiner, Tier 1 Contracts. |

---

### 4. Skill Trees (The "Specialization" System)
Players earn **1 Skill Point per level** (30 total). There are 3 branches, each with 10 skills.

#### **Branch A: The Industrialist (Crafting & Efficiency)**
*Focus: Reducing resource costs and increasing output.*
1.  **Waste Not:** 5% chance to not consume Ore when crafting.
2.  **Rapid Assembly:** Crafting timers reduced by 10%.
3.  **Bulk Processing:** Refiners produce 15% more material per cycle.
4.  **Synergy - "Master Smith":** If Branch B is also at Level 5, unlock "Instant Craft" for Tier 1 items.

#### **Branch B: The Navigator (Exploration & Speed)**
*Focus: Fuel efficiency and reaching new resource nodes faster.*
1.  **Light Footprint:** Reduce Fuel-Cell consumption by 10%.
2.  **Engine Overclock:** Travel speed between sectors increased by 15%.
3.  **Sensor Sweep:** 10% higher chance to find rare Isotope Shards.
4.  **Synergy - "Pathfinder":** If Branch C is also at Level 5, unlock "Warp Jump" (Instant travel to visited sectors).

#### **Branch C: The Tycoon (Logistics & Revenue)**
*Focus: Maximizing Credit gain and storage.*
1.  **Cargo Compression:** Increase ship storage capacity by 20%.
2.  **Contract Negotiation:** Earn 10% more Credits from all deliveries.
3.  **Bulk Discounts:** Blueprint costs in the shop reduced by 15%.
4.  **Synergy - "Market Corner":** If Branch A is also at Level 5, unlock "Priority Shipping" (Double XP from contracts).

---

### 5. Progression Balance & Mechanics

#### **Early Game (Levels 1-10): The Hook**
*   **Rhythm:** Rapid-fire unlocks. Every level provides a new blueprint or ship part.
*   **Goal:** Establish a basic loop: Mine Ore $\rightarrow$ Craft Components $\rightarrow$ Complete Contracts $\rightarrow$ Level Up.

#### **Mid Game (Levels 11-20): The Optimization**
*   **Rhythm:** Progression slows. Players must now manage Isotope Shards (rarer) and Fuel-Cells.
*   **Goal:** Automate the Ore collection so the player can focus on high-tier crafting and exploring dangerous sectors.

#### **Late Game (Levels 21-30): The Mastery**
*   **Rhythm:** Long-term goals. Levels require significant Data Slate (XP) accumulation.
*   **Goal:** Building the "Galactic Command" ship. Players must optimize their skill tree synergies to handle the massive resource requirements of end-game blueprints.

#### **Respec Mechanics**
*   **The "Neural Reset":** Players can reset their skill tree at any time.
*   **Cost:** The cost is **10% of current Galactic Credits**. This makes respecs "cheap" for players who have managed their economy poorly (helping them fix it) but "expensive" for wealthy players, preventing them from constantly swapping skills for every single task.

#### **Casual vs. Hardcore Balance**
*   **Casuals:** Benefit from the **Linear XP curve**. They never feel like they've hit an exponential "wall." Even 20 minutes of play guarantees progress toward the next level.
*   **Hardcore:** Benefit from **Skill Synergies**. By carefully balancing points across two trees, hardcore players can unlock "Warp Jumps" or "Instant Crafting," allowing them to progress through the late-game tiers 2-3x faster than a non-optimized player.

## Loot & Reward System Design

This loot and reward system is designed for **Galactic Trader**, a strategy game where progression is linear and driven by resource management and crafting. Since "Trading" is disabled in the traditional sense, the "Trader" aspect focuses on **optimizing supply chains and converting loot into high-value ship components.**

---

### 1. Loot Tables by Content Type

#### A. Common Activities (Asteroid Mining, Scout Skirmishes)
*Focus: High-volume, low-value materials for base crafting and fuel.*
*   **Item Types & Rarities:**
    *   **Star-Iron Ore (Common):** 70% weight.
    *   **Plasma Trace (Common):** 20% weight.
    *   **Basic Circuitry (Rare):** 8% weight.
    *   **Data Fragment (Rare):** 2% weight.
*   **Scaling:** Yield increases by 15% per Sector Level.
*   **Pity System:** "Scrap Guarantee" – Every 10th common activity without a Rare drop guarantees 2x Basic Circuitry.

#### B. Elite Enemies (Pirate Captains, Rogue Drones)
*Focus: Specialized components required for mid-tier upgrades.*
*   **Item Types & Rarities:**
    *   **Refined Star-Iron (Common):** 50% weight.
    *   **Plasma Cells (Rare):** 35% weight.
    *   **Encrypted Data Shard (Rare):** 10% weight.
    *   **Prototype Weapon Frame (Epic):** 5% weight.
*   **Scaling:** Elites gain "Affixes" at higher levels; each Affix adds a 5% chance to roll the Epic table twice.
*   **Pity System:** "Data Recovery" – If no Data Shard drops in 5 Elite kills, the next one is guaranteed.

#### C. Boss Encounters (Sector Overlords, Ancient Guardians)
*Focus: Major progression leaps and unique crafting blueprints.*
*   **Item Types & Rarities:**
    *   **Bulk Plasma/Iron Bundles (Rare):** 100% (Guaranteed base).
    *   **Advanced Tech Core (Epic):** 80% weight.
    *   **Ancient Artifact (Legendary):** 15% weight.
    *   **Unique Ship Blueprint (Legendary):** 5% weight.
*   **Scaling:** Bosses drop "Sector Tokens" used to bypass crafting timers in the current sector.
*   **Pity System:** "The Blueprint Mercy" – If a Legendary Blueprint hasn't dropped in 3 Boss kills, the 4th kill allows the player to choose one of three random Legendary Blueprints.

#### D. Quest Rewards (Main Story & Side Contracts)
*Focus: Controlled progression and narrative milestones.*
*   **Item Types & Rarities:**
    *   **Credits (Currency):** Fixed amount based on difficulty.
    *   **Data Shards (Experience):** Fixed amount.
    *   **Crafting Kits (Rare/Epic):** Contains all materials needed for the next linear upgrade.
*   **Scaling:** Rewards are pegged to the player’s current "Tech Tier" to ensure they are always relevant.

#### E. Achievement Rewards (Milestones & Feats)
*Focus: Long-term goals and "Premium" feel.*
*   **Item Types & Rarities:**
    *   **Cosmetic Hull Liveries (Epic):** 100% (Fixed).
    *   **Permanent Efficiency Buffs (Legendary):** e.g., "+5% Plasma Extraction."
    *   **Massive Credit Injections:** To clear progression bottlenecks.

---

### 2. Reward Structures

#### Quest Completion
*   **Main Quests:** Provide "Key Components." These are non-random items required to unlock the next Sector. This prevents players from getting stuck due to bad RNG.
*   **Side Contracts:** Provide "Resource Packs." Players can choose between an "Iron Pack," "Energy Pack," or "Data Pack" upon completion, allowing them to fill specific gaps in their economy.

#### Achievement Unlocks
*   **Tiered Achievements:** (e.g., Mine 1k/10k/100k Iron).
*   **Rewards:** Each tier grants a "Production Multiplier."
    *   *Bronze:* +2% yield.
    *   *Silver:* +5% yield.
    *   *Gold:* A "Mastery Part" used to craft the highest-tier gear.

#### Milestone Rewards (Linear Progression)
As the player reaches specific "Net Worth" or "Tech Level" milestones:
*   **Level 5:** Unlock "Auto-Collector" (Reduces manual clicking).
*   **Level 10:** Unlock "Refinery" (Converts Common resources to Rare at a 10:1 ratio).
*   **Level 20:** Unlock "Jump Drive" (Access to higher-tier loot tables).

#### First-Time Bonuses
*   **First Elite Kill:** Guaranteed Epic Weapon Frame to give a power spike.
*   **First Sector Clear:** Large cache of Data Shards to jumpstart the next tier's research.
*   **First Craft:** Refund of 50% of materials spent to encourage experimentation with the crafting system.

---

### 3. System Mechanics & Balance

#### Scaling with Difficulty
To prevent early-game items from becoming obsolete, **Galactic Trader** uses a **"Component Upcycling"** logic. 
*   Loot from Sector 1 (Star-Iron) can be compressed into "High-Tensile Plates" needed for Sector 3. 
*   This ensures that even if a player returns to a low-level area, the loot still contributes to their high-level goals.

#### Anti-Frustration Features (Bad Luck Protection)
*   **The "Entropy" Meter:** A hidden counter for every rarity. Every time a loot roll fails to hit a Rare/Epic/Legendary, the probability for the next roll increases by a fixed percentage (e.g., +1% for Rare, +0.5% for Epic). This resets upon a successful drop.
*   **Resource Transmutation:** If a player has an excess of one resource but zero of another due to RNG, the "Black Market" (a menu-based sink) allows them to exchange resources at a 4:1 ratio.

#### Value Proposition
*   **Short-term:** Common loot provides the "fuel" to keep playing (Plasma) and basic repairs (Iron).
*   **Long-term:** Epic and Legendary drops are the only way to increase "Cargo Capacity" and "Jump Range," which are the primary gates for linear progression.

### Summary of Loot Flow
1.  **Acquire:** Kill/Mine (RNG-based loot).
2.  **Stabilize:** Quests/Milestones (Fixed-reward injections).
3.  **Refine:** Crafting (Convert low-tier loot to high-tier gear).
4.  **Progress:** Use gear to reach the next Sector with better loot tables.

## Monetization Strategy Design

This monetization strategy is designed for **Galactic Trader**, a linear-progression strategy game. Since the game focuses on crafting and resource management rather than player-versus-player (PvP) trading, the monetization focuses on **convenience, aesthetics, and accelerated progression** without compromising the core challenge for non-paying players.

---

### 1. Optional Purchases (The "Galactic Exchange")

These purchases are designed to remove friction or provide long-term value for dedicated players.

*   **Convenience Items:**
    *   **Cargo Bay Expansions:** Increases the storage limit for Raw Materials and Crafted Goods.
        *   *Price:* $2.00 per tier.
        *   *Value:* Reduces the frequency of inventory management, allowing for longer mining/crafting sessions.
    *   **Automated Collection Drones:** A permanent unlock that automatically collects nearby floating debris.
        *   *Price:* $4.99.
        *   *Value:* High QoL (Quality of Life) improvement that reduces repetitive clicking.
*   **Time Savers:**
    *   **Refinery Overclock (7 Days):** Increases crafting speed by 25%.
        *   *Price:* $3.00 (or 300 Nova Crystals).
        *   *Value:* Appeals to players with limited playtime who want to see their "Linear Progression" move faster.
    *   **Resource Doubler (Consumable):** Doubles the yield of the next 10 asteroid harvests.
        *   *Price:* $0.99 for a pack of 5.
*   **Premium Currency (Nova Crystals):**
    *   Used to bypass wait times or buy high-end cosmetics.
    *   *Price Points:* $0.99 (100) to $99.99 (12,000).

---

### 2. Battle Pass: "The Star-Charter’s Log"

The Battle Pass provides a steady stream of rewards to keep players engaged throughout the linear progression.

*   **Duration:** 60 Days (Seasonal).
*   **Price:** $9.99 (Premium) / $24.99 (Elite Bundle with 20 level skip).
*   **Free Tier Rewards:**
    *   Basic Raw Materials (Star-Iron).
    *   Small amounts of Credits.
    *   1 Exclusive "Common" Ship Decal.
    *   Occasional "Data Cores" to help with tech progression.
*   **Premium Tier Rewards:**
    *   **Exclusive Ship Hull:** A unique ship model (purely cosmetic).
    *   **Nova Crystals:** Totaling 1,000 (enough to buy the next pass if saved).
    *   **Crafting Boosters:** Non-stackable speed increases.
    *   **Legendary Engine Trail:** (e.g., Rainbow or Void-Purple exhaust).
*   **Completion Time:** Estimated 40–50 hours of gameplay over 2 months.
*   **Value Proposition:** Provides over $50 worth of items and currency for $10, incentivizing daily logins and mission completion.

---

### 3. Cosmetics: "The Nebula Boutique"

Cosmetics allow players to express their identity as a legendary trader without affecting the stats of their ship.

*   **Categories:**
    *   **Ship Skins:** Full texture overhauls (e.g., "Golden Merchant," "Rusty Scrapper").
    *   **Engine Trails:** Changes the color and particle effects of the ship's thrusters.
    *   **Cockpit Bobbleheads:** Interior decorations visible during flight/menus.
    *   **Station Themes:** Changes the UI and background of the player’s home base.
*   **Rarity Tiers:**
    *   **Common (Blue):** Simple color swaps ($1.00).
    *   **Rare (Purple):** Patterned skins and glowing decals ($3.00).
    *   **Epic (Orange):** Animated textures and minor model changes ($7.00).
    *   **Legendary (Gold):** Unique geometry, custom sound effects, and unique docking animations ($15.00).
*   **Acquisition:** Direct purchase via the Boutique or as milestone rewards in the Battle Pass.

---

### 4. Pay-to-Win (P2W) Risk Assessment

 Purchase Type | P2W Risk | Mitigation Strategy |
 :--- | :--- | :--- |
 **Resource Doublers** | Medium | Since there is no PvP and no player-to-player trading, "winning" is subjective. However, to prevent players from "skipping the game," doublers are capped at a maximum usage per day. |
 **Inventory Space** | Low | This is a pure QoL feature. It doesn't make the ship stronger, only more efficient at hauling. |
 **Data Core Packs** | High | Selling the "Experience/Tech" resource directly could ruin the linear progression. **Safeguard:** Data Cores cannot be bought directly; they must be earned through gameplay or the Battle Pass. |
 **Crafting Speed-ups** | Low | Speed-ups only reduce real-world waiting time. They do not provide better gear than what a F2P player can craft. |

**Final Safeguard Recommendation:**
To maintain balance, ensure that the **best-in-slot equipment** (the highest tier of crafted ships and engines) can *only* be obtained by spending **Data Cores** earned through gameplay milestones. This ensures that while a paying player might get there *faster*, they cannot bypass the requirement to actually play the game's content to reach the "End Game" status.

## Engagement Systems Design

This engagement design for **Galactic Trader** complements the core economy (Credits, Raw Ore, Tech Components, and Fuel) by providing structured goals and social incentives. Since player-to-player trading is disabled, these systems focus on **personal progression milestones** and **collaborative community goals**.

---

### 1. Daily Rewards: "The Captain’s Log"
The Daily Reward system is designed to build a "habit loop" by rewarding the player for the simple act of docking at a Starbase.

*   **Reward Structure:** A 7-day rotating calendar.
    *   *Days 1-3:* Small bundles of **Fuel** and **Raw Ore**.
    *   *Days 4-6:* **Tech Components** and **Credits**.
    *   *Day 7:* "High-Yield Crate" containing a random Blueprint or a large sum of Credits.
*   **Streak Bonuses:** Every 7-day cycle completed without a break increases a "Loyalty Multiplier" (up to 1.5x) applied to all daily rewards.
*   **Catch-up Mechanics:** Players can spend a modest amount of **Credits** (the primary currency sink) to "Recover" a missed day and maintain their streak.
*   **Value Scaling:** Rewards are not fixed; they scale based on the player’s **Trade Rank**. A Rank 10 player receives 10x the resources of a Rank 1 player, ensuring the rewards remain relevant throughout the linear progression.
*   **Retention Impact:** High short-term retention. It creates a "loss aversion" hook regarding the streak multiplier.

---

### 2. Seasonal Content: "Galactic Cycles"
To prevent the linear progression from feeling stagnant, Seasonal Content introduces temporary shifts in the economy.

*   **Season Duration:** 60 Days (6 seasons per year).
*   **Seasonal Themes:**
    *   *Example: "The Silicon Gold Rush"* – Increases the value of Tech Components and introduces "Corrupted Circuits" as a seasonal crafting material.
*   **Exclusive Rewards:**
    *   **Cosmetic:** Unique ship hull patterns and engine trail colors.
    *   **Functional:** "Legacy Blueprints" for ships with unique utility slots (e.g., +10% Fuel Efficiency) that cannot be crafted once the season ends.
*   **Seasonal Progression:** A "Season Pass" style track where players earn **Prestige XP** by completing trade routes and crafting specific seasonal items.
*   **Balance with Core Gameplay:** Seasonal materials are converted into **Raw Ore** at the end of the season to prevent inventory clutter and long-term inflation.

---

### 3. Events: "Sector Anomalies"
Events provide short-term spikes in activity and encourage players to deviate from their "optimal" trade routes.

*   **Event Types:**
    *   **Flash Demand (Recurring):** Every Tuesday/Thursday, a specific Starbase offers 300% Credits for a specific crafted item for 4 hours.
    *   **Deep Space Discovery (Limited-time):** A 3-day event where "Unstable Wormholes" appear, leading to resource-rich zones with high Fuel costs but massive Raw Ore yields.
    *   **Community Megastructure (Special):** A month-long event where all players contribute Tech Components to build a "Galactic Gateway."
*   **Event Frequency:** Weekly minor events; Monthly major events.
*   **Participation Incentives:** Leaderboards (non-competitive) that grant "Commendations" used to buy permanent upgrades to storage capacity.
*   **Retention Impact:** Medium-term. These events break the monotony of the linear grind and provide "appointment gaming" opportunities.

---

### 4. Retention Mechanics: "Merchant Guilds & Logistics"
Since there is no player-to-player trading, social features focus on **cooperation** and **long-term investment**.

*   **Login Bonuses (The Daily Shipment):** Upon login, players receive a "Shipment Report" showing passive income generated by their automated mining drones (if unlocked), providing immediate positive feedback.
*   **Comeback Rewards (The Insurance Payout):** Players returning after 7+ days receive a "Galactic Insurance Settlement"—a bundle of Fuel and Credits designed to get them back into the trade loops immediately without feeling "behind" the curve.
*   **Merchant Guilds (Social System):**
    *   **Shared Research:** Guild members contribute resources to a "Guild Tech Tree" that provides passive buffs (e.g., -5% Crafting Time) to all members.
    *   **Guild Projects:** Large-scale resource sinks where members work together to "settle" a new planet, unlocking a new trade hub for the entire guild.
*   **Expected Retention Impact:** High long-term retention. The Guild system creates social accountability and a sense of belonging, while the Comeback mechanic reduces the friction of re-entry for churned players.

---

### Summary Table: Engagement System Balance

 System | Frequency | Primary Reward | Retention Impact | Economic Role |
 :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
 **Daily Log** | Daily | Mixed Resources | Short-term (Daily) | Resource Injection |
 **Seasons** | 60 Days | Blueprints/Skins | Long-term (Months) | Progression Variety |
 **Flash Events** | Bi-weekly | Credits | Mid-term (Weekly) | Currency Sink/Source |
 **Guilds** | Permanent | Passive Buffs | Long-term (Years) | Massive Resource Sink |

### Design Philosophy Note:
These systems avoid "pay-to-win" by focusing on **time-based rewards** and **scaling**. Because the game is a linear strategy title, the engagement hooks are designed to make the "grind" feel like a "career." By rewarding consistency (Daily Log) and cooperation (Guilds), *Galactic Trader* ensures that the player's progression feels earned and their presence in the galaxy feels impactful.

### 1. Resource Identity
