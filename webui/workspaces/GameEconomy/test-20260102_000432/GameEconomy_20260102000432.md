# Game Economy Design: Galactic Trader

**Started:** 2026-01-02 00:04:32
## Game Economy Design

**Game:** Galactic Trader

**Type:** strategy

**Progression Style:** linear

## Resource System Design

This design for **Galactic Trader** reinterprets the "Trader" title as a logistics and supply-chain strategy game. Since player-to-player or market trading is disabled, the "trade" occurs through **Contract Fulfillment** (converting raw materials into progression) and **Resource Synthesis** (Crafting).

---

### 1. Resource Identity: Stellar Ore (The Foundation)
*   **Type**: Common Material
*   **Purpose**: The primary building block for all physical assets. It represents the "bulk" of the player's inventory and is the main ingredient in basic crafting.
*   **Thematic Fit**: Raw minerals mined from asteroids, essential for hull plating and basic machinery.

### 2. Resource Identity: Plasma Cells (The Catalyst)
*   **Type**: Utility/Energy Resource
*   **Purpose**: Acts as the "fuel" for both movement and the crafting process. It prevents infinite rapid progression by gating how many actions can be taken or how fast machines can run.
*   **Thematic Fit**: High-energy canisters harvested from gas giants or stars, used to power warp drives and industrial forges.

### 3. Resource Identity: Data Fragments (The Progression Gate)
*   **Type**: Experience/Research Resource
*   **Purpose**: Used exclusively for linear progression. It unlocks new crafting tiers, ship upgrades, and new sectors of space. It cannot be "spent" on consumables, only on permanent unlocks.
*   **Thematic Fit**: Encrypted signals and ancient tech blueprints recovered from derelict ships and anomalies.

### 4. Resource Identity: Dark Matter (The Premium/Rare Catalyst)
*   **Type**: Premium/Rare Material
*   **Purpose**: Used for high-end "Masterwork" crafting and bypassing time-gates. It is the only resource that can bridge the gap between tiers early or provide significant stat boosts to ships.
*   **Thematic Fit**: An exotic, unstable substance found in black hole event horizons; the pinnacle of galactic power.

---

### Generation Sources

 Resource | Primary Source | Amount | Frequency | Scaling |
 :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
 **Stellar Ore** | Asteroid Mining | High (100-500) | Constant (Per minute) | Increases with Drill Upgrades |
 **Plasma Cells** | Solar Siphoning | Medium (20-50) | Periodic (Per jump) | Increases with Tank Capacity |
 **Data Fragments** | Mission Completion | Low (1-5) | Occasional (Per quest) | Fixed per Sector tier |
 **Dark Matter** | Bosses / IAP | Very Low (1) | Rare (Weekly/Milestone) | Does not scale (Fixed value) |

---

### Consumption Uses (Crafting & Progression)

*   **Stellar Ore**: 
    *   *Use*: Crafting "Basic Components" (Gears, Plates).
    *   *Cost*: 500 Ore per component.
    *   *Value*: Necessary for every ship upgrade and mission requirement.
*   **Plasma Cells**: 
    *   *Use*: Powering the "Refinery" and "Warp Drive."
    *   *Cost*: 10 Cells per craft; 50 Cells per sector jump.
    *   *Value*: The "Action Point" equivalent; manages session length and pacing.
*   **Data Fragments**: 
    *   *Use*: "Tech Tree" nodes.
    *   *Cost*: 10–500 Fragments (Exponentially increasing).
    *   *Value*: Unlocks the ability to use higher-tier Ore and Plasma.
*   **Dark Matter**: 
    *   *Use*: "Instant Finish" or "Legendary Modules."
    *   *Cost*: 1–10 Dark Matter.
    *   *Value*: Significant time-saving or unique power spikes.

---

### Storage and Limits

*   **Stellar Ore**: Limited by **Cargo Bay** capacity. Excess ore is vented into space (lost). This forces players to choose between hoarding raw ore or crafting it into denser components.
*   **Plasma Cells**: Limited by **Battery Banks**. This creates a "soft cap" on how many missions a player can do in one sitting.
*   **Data Fragments**: **No Limit**. As a progression resource, capping it would feel punitive to linear growth.
*   **Dark Matter**: **No Limit**. Being a rare/premium resource, players are encouraged to accumulate it.

---

### Exchange Rates (The "Internal Trade" System)

Since there is no external trading, the player "trades" with the game systems:
*   **Synthesis**: 1,000 Stellar Ore + 50 Plasma Cells → 1 **Advanced Alloy** (A higher-tier crafting material).
*   **Data Extraction**: 5 Advanced Alloys → 1 **Data Fragment** (A way to grind for progression if missions are too difficult).
*   **Dark Matter Infusion**: 1 Dark Matter can be "shattered" into a full refill of Plasma Cells and Stellar Ore (Emergency recovery).

---

### Sink Mechanisms & Inflation Prevention

1.  **Maintenance Costs**: As ships get larger (linear progression), they require a flat "Stellar Ore" tax per jump for repairs. This ensures that early-game resources remain relevant and don't accumulate infinitely.
2.  **The "Contract" System**: NPCs offer "Trade Contracts" where they demand massive amounts of crafted components in exchange for a single permanent ship cosmetic or a small amount of Data Fragments. This acts as a massive sink for mid-game materials.
3.  **Exponential Crafting Tiers**: Each new tier of technology requires the previous tier's components. To make a Tier 3 engine, you need 4 Tier 2 engines, which each need 4 Tier 1 engines. This geometric growth naturally consumes any surplus in the economy.
4.  **Plasma Decay**: A small percentage of Plasma Cells leak over time if the Battery Banks are not upgraded, encouraging players to spend energy rather than hoarding it.

### Summary of the Loop
The player **mines** Ore, **siphons** Plasma, and **crafts** them into components to fulfill **Contracts**. Fulfilling Contracts provides **Data Fragments**, which unlock the next tier of the galaxy, where the cycle repeats with higher numbers and more complex recipes. **Dark Matter** acts as the lubricant to speed up this linear journey.

## Progression System Design

This design for **Galactic Trader** focuses on a "Supply Chain Strategy" model. Since player-to-player or NPC market trading is disabled, the game focuses on **Resource Extraction, Refinement, and Contract Fulfillment** to drive progression.

---

## Part 1: Resource System Design

### 1. Resource Identity
 Resource | Type | Purpose | Thematic Fit |
 :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
 **Hull Scrap** | Material | Basic building block for ship repairs and base components. | Salvaged from space debris and asteroid belts. |
 **Ion Cells** | Energy | Fuel for travel and the primary "catalyst" for crafting recipes. | Harvested from gas giants and solar arrays. |
 **Neural Data** | Experience | Used to unlock new technologies and level up the player. | Gathered via scanning anomalies and deep-space telemetry. |
 **Galactic Credits** | Currency | Used for hardware upgrades and "Contract Licensing." | Earned by fulfilling government supply mandates. |

### 2. Generation Sources
*   **Hull Scrap:** Acquired via Mining Lasers (50-100 per node). Frequency: High. Scales with "Drill Tier."
*   **Ion Cells:** Acquired via Siphon Drones (20-40 per minute). Frequency: Passive/Steady. Scales with "Siphon Efficiency."
*   **Neural Data:** Acquired via Exploration Scans (500 per anomaly). Frequency: Low/Burst. Scales with "Sensor Range."
*   **Galactic Credits:** Acquired by "Turning In" crafted goods to fulfill Contracts. Amount varies by item complexity.

### 3. Consumption Uses
*   **Crafting:** Combining Hull Scrap + Ion Cells to create "Trade Goods" (e.g., Engine Parts, Shield Generators).
*   **Upgrades:** Credits + Hull Scrap are used to increase Cargo Capacity and Speed.
*   **Leveling:** Neural Data is consumed to reach the next Level/Tier.
*   **Maintenance:** Ion Cells are consumed during travel (Sinks).

### 4. Storage and Limits
*   **Storage:** Limited by "Cargo Bays" on the ship.
*   **Rationale:** Forces players to choose which resources to prioritize for their current crafting goal.
*   **Overflow:** Excess resources are automatically converted into a small amount of Credits (5% value) to prevent "stuck" gameplay.

### 5. Exchange Rates (Internal Crafting)
*   **Basic Conversion:** 10 Hull Scrap + 2 Ion Cells = 1 Basic Component.
*   **Advanced Conversion:** 5 Basic Components + 10 Ion Cells = 1 High-Tier Module.
*   **Value Proposition:** Higher-tier modules provide 3x the Credits and 2x the Neural Data when submitted for contracts.

### 6. Sink Mechanisms
*   **Fuel Consumption:** Constant drain of Ion Cells during movement.
*   **Licensing Fees:** Credits are drained to unlock new sectors of space.
*   **Research Decay:** High-level Neural Data unlocks require "Maintenance Data," creating a soft sink for late-game players.

---

## Part 2: Progression System

### Experience Curve (Linear)
The XP (Neural Data) required per level follows a linear formula:
**XP Required = 1,000 + (Level × 1,000)**

*   **Level 1:** 1,000 XP
*   **Level 15:** 15,000 XP
*   **Level 30:** 30,000 XP
*   **Total XP to Max:** 465,000 Neural Data.

### Unlock Schedule & Milestones
 Level | Unlock Category | Specific Unlock | Milestone Reward |
 :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
 **1-5** | **Early Game** | Basic Mining, Tier 1 Hull Plating | **Lvl 5:** "The Scout" Ship (Faster) |
 **6-10** | **Automation** | Siphon Drones, Auto-Refiners | **Lvl 10:** Skill Tree Unlocked |
 **11-20** | **Mid Game** | Tier 2 Components, Warp Drive | **Lvl 20:** "The Freighter" (Huge Cargo) |
 **21-29** | **Late Game** | Exotic Matter Crafting, Deep Space Scans | **Lvl 25:** Sector 4 Access |
 **30** | **End Game** | "Galactic Hub" Construction | **Lvl 30:** Infinite Prestige Rank |

**Estimated Time to Max Level:**
*   **Casual:** 60-80 hours (focusing on passive generation).
*   **Hardcore:** 35-45 hours (optimizing scan routes and crafting chains).

---

## Part 3: Skill Trees

Players earn **1 Skill Point per level** starting at Level 10 (21 points total).

### Branch 1: The Industrialist (Crafting Focus)
*   **Efficiency:** Reduce Hull Scrap cost for all recipes by 5/10/15%.
*   **Overclock:** Crafting speed increased by 20%.
*   **Masterwork:** 10% chance to produce double items.
*   **Synergy:** If "The Hauler" branch is also active, Masterwork items weigh 50% less.

### Branch 2: The Hauler (Logistics Focus)
*   **Expanded Bays:** Increase storage capacity by 10/20/30%.
*   **Ion Streamlining:** Reduce Ion Cell consumption during travel by 25%.
*   **Reinforced Hull:** Immune to environmental damage in asteroid belts.
*   **Synergy:** If "The Industrialist" branch is active, travel speed increases when cargo is full.

### Branch 3: The Analyst (Discovery Focus)
*   **Deep Scan:** Neural Data gain from anomalies increased by 20%.
*   **Resource Pinging:** Reveal high-density Scrap nodes on the map.
*   **Data Mining:** Small chance to find Neural Data while mining Hull Scrap.
*   **Synergy:** If "The Hauler" branch is active, scanning an anomaly restores 10% Fuel (Ion Cells).

### Respec Mechanics
*   **Cost:** Respeccing costs **Galactic Credits**.
*   **Scaling:** The first respec is cheap (1,000 Credits), but the cost doubles each time it is used to prevent constant "build-swapping" and encourage commitment to a path.

---

## Design Summary
This system creates a **Linear Progression** where the player's "Trading" is actually a sophisticated **Logistics Loop**. By removing player-to-player trading, the economy remains stable and predictable, allowing the developer to balance the 30-level journey precisely. Players must balance their **Ion Cell** consumption (travel) against their **Hull Scrap** needs (crafting) to generate the **Neural Data** required to reach the next tier of the galaxy.

## Loot & Reward System Design

This design builds upon the **Galactic Trader** economy, focusing on a "Harvest-Craft-Upgrade" loop. Since player-to-player trading is disabled, the loot system serves as the primary driver for progression, ensuring players have the necessary **Raw Ore** and **Refined Isotopes** to spend their **Star-Credits** and **Command XP**.

---

### 1. Resource Recap
*   **Star-Credits (Currency):** Used for repairs, fuel, and purchasing blueprints.
*   **Raw Ore (Material):** Common drop; used for basic hull and engine upgrades.
*   **Refined Isotopes (Material):** Rare drop; used for high-tier tech and specialized modules.
*   **Command XP (Experience):** Earned through combat and discovery; unlocks new ship tiers.

---

### 2. Loot Tables & Drop Logic

#### A. Common Activities (Asteroid Mining / Scout Drones)
*Focus: High-volume, low-rarity materials to sustain basic crafting.*
*   **Common (80%):** Raw Ore (10–20 units), Scrap Metal (used for basic repairs).
*   **Rare (15%):** Small Star-Credit Cache (50–100 Credits), Refined Isotope (1 unit).
*   **Epic (5%):** Basic Component Blueprint (e.g., Improved Cargo Bay).
*   **Scaling:** Yield increases by 10% per Sector Level.

#### B. Elite Enemies (Pirate Captains / Rogue Sentinels)
*Focus: Guaranteed progression materials and mid-tier upgrades.*
*   **Common (50%):** Large Raw Ore Bundle (50+ units).
*   **Rare (35%):** Refined Isotopes (3–5 units), Mid-tier Star-Credit Cache.
*   **Epic (12%):** Advanced Tech Blueprints (e.g., Shield Overcharger).
*   **Legendary (3%):** Prototype Component (Pre-crafted high-tier part).
*   **Pity System:** If no Refined Isotopes drop for 3 Elite kills, the 4th is guaranteed to drop a "Refined Isotope Cluster" (10 units).

#### C. Boss Encounters (Sector Overlords / Ancient Guardians)
*Focus: Major progression milestones and unique items.*
*   **Rare (100%):** Large Refined Isotope Bundle (15+ units).
*   **Epic (70%):** Rare Blueprint (Specific to the Boss type).
*   **Legendary (30%):** "Core Module" (Unique, non-craftable ship part).
*   **Guaranteed:** Massive Command XP boost (enough to fill 25% of current level).
*   **Bad Luck Protection:** Bosses drop "Data Fragments." 5 Fragments can be exchanged for any Legendary Blueprint from that Sector’s loot pool.

---

### 3. Reward Structures

#### Quest Completion (The Narrative Path)
Quests are the primary source of **Command XP** and **Star-Credits** to ensure the player never gets "stuck" in a linear progression.
*   **Primary Quests:** Large amounts of Command XP, guaranteed Blueprints for the next ship tier, and enough Star-Credits to cover 50% of the next upgrade cost.
*   **Side Quests:** Focused on specific resources (e.g., "The Isotope Shortage" quest rewards 20 Refined Isotopes).
*   **Repeatable Contracts:** Small, steady streams of Star-Credits and Raw Ore to prevent "soft-locking" if a player loses their ship.

#### Achievement Unlocks (The Skill Path)
Achievements provide "Quality of Life" (QoL) rewards rather than raw materials.
*   **"Master Miner" (Mine 10,000 Ore):** Permanent +5% Raw Ore yield.
*   **"Ace Pilot" (Destroy 100 Elites):** Permanent -10% Star-Credit cost for ship repairs.
*   **"Deep Space Explorer" (Visit all Sectors):** Unlocks a unique Legendary Ship Skin.

#### Milestone Rewards (The Growth Path)
Triggered when the player reaches specific Command XP levels.
*   **Level 5, 10, 15...:** "Command Gift" containing a choice of three "Resource Packs" (e.g., Pack A: 500 Credits; Pack B: 100 Raw Ore; Pack C: 10 Refined Isotopes). This allows players to fill whatever gap is currently stalling their progression.

#### First-Time Bonuses (The Exploration Path)
*   **First Elite Kill:** Guaranteed Epic Blueprint drop.
*   **First Sector Jump:** 1,000 Star-Credit "Discovery Bonus."
*   **First Crafted Item:** Refund of 50% of the materials used to encourage experimentation with the crafting system.

---

### 4. System Balancing & Scaling

 Progression Stage | Primary Loot Source | Scaling Mechanic |
 :--- | :--- | :--- |
 **Early Game** | Common Activities | High drop rates for Raw Ore to teach crafting basics. |
 **Mid Game** | Elite Enemies | Shift toward Refined Isotopes; Star-Credit sinks increase. |
 **Late Game** | Bosses / Achievements | Focus on Legendary Blueprints and Command XP for Tier-5 Ships. |

**Anti-Frustration Mechanics:**
1.  **The Scrapper:** Players can "deconstruct" any unwanted Epic or Legendary Blueprints to receive 50% of the Refined Isotopes required to craft them. This ensures that "bad" drops still contribute to the player's specific goals.
2.  **Linear Scaling:** Loot quantity is tied to the "Sector Level." If a player returns to a low-level sector, the loot quantity is capped, encouraging them to move forward into more dangerous (and rewarding) space.
3.  **Inventory Overflow:** If the player's cargo hold is full, excess Raw Ore is automatically converted into a small amount of Command XP (at a 10:1 ratio), ensuring that no activity feels like a waste of time.

### 5. Summary of Value Proposition
*   **Short-term:** Mining and Scout kills provide the Raw Ore needed for immediate repairs and fuel.
*   **Mid-term:** Elite hunting and Side Quests provide the Refined Isotopes and Blueprints needed to upgrade ship modules.
*   **Long-term:** Bosses and Command XP milestones unlock new ship classes, allowing the player to progress to the next sector in the linear story.

## Monetization Strategy Design

This monetization strategy is designed for **Galactic Trader**, a linear progression strategy game. Since player-to-player trading is disabled, the economy is a "closed loop," which makes it easier to prevent pay-to-win (P2W) imbalances while focusing on player expression and convenience.

---

### 1. Optional Purchases
Optional purchases focus on removing friction and allowing players to express their identity within the galaxy.

 Item Type | Description | Price Point (Est. USD) | Perceived Value |
 :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
 **Hangar Expansion** | Increases storage for crafted components and raw materials. | $2.99 per 20 slots | High: Reduces the need for inventory management "chore" time. |
 **Automated Scavengers** | Drones that collect Scrap Metal automatically while the player is offline (capped). | $4.99 | Medium: Great for casual players with limited playtime. |
 **Jump-Gate Pass** | Permanent unlock for fast travel between major sectors (normally costs Plasma Cells). | $9.99 | High: Significant time-saver for late-game backtracking. |
 **Resource Overclock** | 2x Resource acquisition (Scrap/Plasma) for 2 hours of active play. | $0.99 / 5-pack $3.99 | Medium: Helps players push through a specific crafting grind. |
 **Dark Matter Packs** | Premium currency used for cosmetics and skipping crafting timers. | $4.99 - $99.99 | Variable: The "engine" for all other premium transactions. |

---

### 2. Battle Pass: "The Star-Charter’s Log"
The Battle Pass provides a seasonal progression track that rewards consistent play.

*   **Duration:** 60 Days (Seasonal).
*   **Price:** 1,000 Dark Matter (~$9.99). A "Commanders Bundle" for 2,400 Dark Matter skips the first 20 tiers.
*   **Free Tier Rewards:** Basic Scrap Metal bundles, small amounts of Credits, 1 "Standard" Ship Hull, and 200 Dark Matter (allowing free players to eventually save up for a Premium Pass).
*   **Premium Tier Rewards:** Exclusive "Void-Steel" Ship Skins, unique Engine Trail effects, 800 Dark Matter (rebate), and "Blueprint Fragments" for unique (but balanced) ship modules.
*   **Estimated Completion Time:** 40–50 hours of active gameplay.
*   **Value Proposition:** Provides over $50 worth of items and currency for $10, encouraging daily engagement and long-term retention.

---

### 3. Cosmetics
Cosmetics are the primary revenue driver, allowing players to customize their fleet without affecting combat or trading stats.

**Categories:**
*   **Hull Wraps (Skins):** Full-body textures and patterns for ships.
*   **Engine Glows:** Changes the color and particle effects of the ship's thrusters.
*   **Cockpit Bobbleheads:** Interactive interior decorations visible during flight.
*   **UI Themes:** Custom HUD colors and sound-effect packs (e.g., "Retro-Tech" or "Alien Bio-Organic").

**Rarity Tiers & Pricing:**
*   **Common (Green):** Simple color swaps. (Direct buy: $1.50)
*   **Rare (Blue):** Patterned skins with minor glow effects. (Direct buy: $5.00)
*   **Epic (Purple):** Animated textures (e.g., shifting nebula patterns). (Direct buy: $10.00)
*   **Legendary (Gold):** Unique 3D model geometry changes (e.g., adding extra wings or rotating rings). (Direct buy: $18.00)

---

### 4. Pay-to-Win (P2W) Risk Assessment

 Purchase Type | P2W Risk Level | Potential Issue | Recommended Safeguard |
 :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
 **Resource Boosters** | **Low** | Players might out-level content too quickly, making the strategy trivial. | Boosters only affect *quantity* of common drops, not the *quality* of rare blueprints found in missions. |
 **Crafting Skips** | **Medium** | Players can instantly acquire high-tier gear by spending Dark Matter. | Implement a "Tech Level" gate. Players cannot skip a craft if they haven't reached the required progression milestone. |
 **Inventory Space** | **Low** | More space allows for more efficient crafting loops. | Ensure the base inventory is generous enough that expansion feels like a "luxury" rather than a "necessity." |
 **Battle Pass Blueprints** | **Medium** | Exclusive gear could be more powerful than base-game gear. | Battle Pass blueprints must be "Side-grades" (e.g., +10% Speed but -10% Armor) rather than "Up-grades." |

**Final Safeguard Recommendation:**
To maintain the integrity of the linear progression, **Galactic Trader** should implement a **"Hard-Cap Milestone"** system. No amount of spending should allow a player to acquire "Tier 3" technology while they are still in the "Tier 1" sector of the galaxy. This ensures that the strategy and challenge of the game remain intact for all players, regardless of their spending habits.

## Engagement Systems Design

This engagement system design for **Galactic Trader** is built to complement a linear progression strategy game where crafting is central and player-to-player trading is disabled. The focus is on rewarding consistency, providing mid-term goals through seasons, and fostering a sense of community through non-market social features.

---

### 1. Daily Rewards: The "Logistics Log"
The Logistics Log rewards players for maintaining their trade routes and checking in on their fleet operations.

*   **Reward Structure**: A 28-day monthly grid.
    *   *Days 1-6*: Incremental amounts of **Raw Ore** and **Credits**.
    *   *Day 7 (Milestone)*: A "Tech Cache" containing **Tech Fragments**.
    *   *Day 28 (Final)*: A small amount of **Dark Matter** (Premium) or a unique Crafting Blueprint.
*   **Streak Bonuses**: Every 7 consecutive days, players receive a "Production Overclock" (a 2-hour 2x speed boost to crafting).
*   **Catch-up Mechanics**: If a day is missed, players can spend a scaling amount of **Credits** (soft currency) to "Back-fill" the log. This prevents the "all-is-lost" feeling of a broken streak.
*   **Value Scaling**: Rewards are grouped by "Trade Rank" (Player Level). A Rank 10 player receives 5,000 Credits on Day 1, while a Rank 50 player receives 50,000, ensuring the rewards remain relevant throughout the linear progression.
*   **Retention Impact**: High short-term retention (D1-D7). It establishes a daily habit of opening the app.

---

### 2. Seasonal Content: "The Star-Path"
Seasons provide a thematic wrapper for the linear progression, giving veteran players new goals and new players a sense of a living world.

*   **Season Duration**: 60 days (2 months), followed by a 7-day "Intermission."
*   **Seasonal Themes**: Themes rotate between "Industrial Revolution" (focus on Ore/Crafting), "Ancient Discovery" (focus on Tech Fragments), and "Void Expansion" (focus on exploration).
*   **Exclusive Rewards**: 
    *   Cosmetic ship hulls and engine trail colors.
    *   "Legacy Blueprints": Crafting recipes that are slightly more efficient than standard ones but only unlockable during the season.
*   **Seasonal Progression**: A 50-tier progression track. Players earn "Season XP" by completing standard crafting tasks and missions. 
    *   *Free Track*: Basic materials and Credits.
    *   *Premium Track*: Dark Matter, exclusive Blueprints, and instant-complete tokens.
*   **Retention Impact**: High mid-term retention (D30-D60). It prevents the "content plateau" common in linear strategy games.

---

### 3. Events: "Sector Anomalies"
Events break the routine of the linear grind by introducing temporary rules or high-value targets.

*   **Event Types**:
    *   **Asteroid Pulse (Recurring - Weekends)**: 2x yield on Raw Ore harvesting.
    *   **Research Breakthrough (Limited-time - 3 days)**: 30% reduction in Tech Fragment costs for specific tech branches.
    *   **The Great Construction (Special - 1 week)**: A community-wide goal to craft components for a "Mega-Station." While players don't trade with each other, their individual crafting contributions fill a global bar.
*   **Event Frequency**: One minor event every weekend; one major "Special" event once per month.
*   **Participation Incentives**: Leaderboards based on "Value Crafted" during the event. Top 10% receive unique badges and bulk resources.
*   **Balance with Core Gameplay**: Events are designed to drain resource stockpiles (Sinks) by encouraging players to craft heavily in exchange for exclusive rewards.

---

### 4. Retention Mechanics
These systems focus on long-term player loyalty and re-engaging players who have drifted away.

*   **Login Bonuses (The "Daily Shipment")**:
    *   Separate from the Logistics Log, this is a "Welcome Back" gift that accumulates while the player is offline (up to 24 hours). It reinforces the "Strategy/Idle" hybrid feel.
*   **Comeback Rewards ("Deep Space Signal")**:
    *   Triggered after 14+ days of inactivity.
    *   Upon return, the player receives a "Refurbished Fleet" buff (50% faster crafting for 48 hours) and a bundle of resources equal to 25% of what they would have earned if they had logged in daily.
*   **Social Features: "Trade Federations" (Guilds)**:
    *   Since there is no trading, Federations focus on **Shared Research**. Members contribute Tech Fragments to a "Federation Tech Tree" that provides passive buffs (e.g., +5% cargo space) to all members.
    *   **Federation Missions**: Cooperative PvE challenges where members must collectively craft a certain number of high-tier items to unlock a reward for everyone.
*   **Expected Retention Impact**: 
    *   *Social*: Increases long-term "stickiness" by creating social obligation and collective goals.
    *   *Comeback*: Reduces churn by lowering the barrier to re-entry for lapsed players.

---

### Summary of Engagement Balance

 System | Frequency | Primary Reward | Retention Goal |
 :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
 **Logistics Log** | Daily | Credits / Tech Fragments | Daily Habit (D1-D7) |
 **Star-Path** | 60 Days | Blueprints / Cosmetics | Mid-term Depth (D30+) |
 **Sector Anomalies** | Weekly | Resource Multipliers | Short-term Spikes |
 **Trade Federations** | Continuous | Passive Buffs | Long-term Community |

**Design Philosophy Note:** Because **Galactic Trader** lacks player-to-player trading, these systems focus heavily on **Crafting Throughput**. Every reward is designed to either provide the materials for crafting or speed up the crafting process, ensuring the core loop remains the primary driver of player enjoyment.

## Economy Forecast (6 months)

This design for **Galactic Trader** focuses on a closed-loop economy where the player progresses through increasingly dangerous sectors. Since P2P trading is disabled, the economy functions as a "Resource Funnel," where raw inputs are refined into progression milestones.

---

# Part 1: Resource System Design

### 1. Galactic Credits (GC) - [Currency]
*   **Identity**: The universal medium of exchange. Used for operational costs and NPC transactions.
*   **Generation**: Selling cargo to NPC stations, completing bounty contracts, and clearing sector waypoints.
*   **Consumption**: Fuel, hull repairs, crew wages, and purchasing "Base Components" that cannot be mined.
*   **Storage**: Unlimited digital wallet.
*   **Sink**: Scaling "Sector Taxes" and high-frequency consumables (Fuel/Ammo).

### 2. Asteroid Ore (AO) - [Raw Material]
*   **Identity**: The bulk material found in planetary rings. The foundation of all physical construction.
*   **Generation**: Mining mini-games and automated extractor drones.
*   **Consumption**: Refined into Nano-Circuitry or used directly for basic ship repairs and hull plating.
*   **Storage**: Limited by Ship Cargo Hold capacity. Requires upgrades to carry more.
*   **Sink**: Bulk construction of Space Stations and "Ship Scrapping" for minor XP.

### 3. Nano-Circuitry (NC) - [Crafted Material]
*   **Identity**: High-value components created by processing Ore. Represents the player's industrial capability.
*   **Generation**: Crafted in the onboard fabricator (Requires: AO + GC + Time).
*   **Consumption**: Upgrading ship modules (Engines, Shields, Lasers) and building advanced trade beacons.
*   **Storage**: Specialized "Secure Storage" slots (limited capacity to prevent hoarding).
*   **Sink**: High-tier equipment failure/degradation and "Tech Donations" for faction standing.

### 4. Research Fragments (RF) - [Progression/Experience]
*   **Identity**: Data recovered from ancient derelicts or black hole horizons.
*   **Generation**: Exploration of "Uncharted Zones," scanning anomalies, and rare mission rewards.
*   **Consumption**: Spent in the Tech Tree to unlock new crafting recipes and ship classes.
*   **Storage**: Data Bank (capped per Sector to enforce linear progression).
*   **Sink**: Once the Tech Tree is maxed, RF can be "Overclocked" to provide temporary 24-hour buffs to mining or speed.

## Balance Report

This report provides a comprehensive balance analysis for **Galactic Trader**, a linear-progression strategy game. The economy is built on four pillars: **Plasma Cores** (Action/Energy), **Void Shards** (Crafting/Material), **Trade Bonds** (Currency), and **Exo-Data** (Experience/Progression).

---

### 1. Resource Balance
**Analysis:**
*   **Generation vs. Consumption:** The economy follows a "Faucet-Drain" model. **Plasma Cores** act as the primary gate for session length, regenerating at a rate that allows for three 20-minute sessions per day. **Void Shards** have a lower generation rate (scarcity) to ensure crafting feels impactful.
*   **Storage Limits:** Storage for **Trade Bonds** is uncapped to allow for long-term saving, but **Void Shards** and **Plasma Cores** have strict caps. This prevents "hoarding" which would trivialize future content updates.
*   **Exchange Rates:** Since P2P trading is disabled, the system uses a "Black Market" NPC. The exchange rate is intentionally inefficient (3:1 ratio) to encourage players to play the specific content designed for a resource rather than just farming the easiest path.
*   **Sink Effectiveness:** Ship maintenance and "Fuel Taxes" (Plasma Core sinks) effectively remove 15% of daily earnings, preventing hyper-inflation of Trade Bonds.

### 2. Progression Balance
**Analysis:**
*   **XP Curve (Exo-Data):** The progression is linear but uses a "Staircase" model. Every 5 levels, the Exo-Data requirement increases by 40%, coinciding with the need to craft a new Ship Tier.
*   **Unlock Pacing:** New sectors unlock every 10 levels. The balance ensures that a player who only does "Main Quests" will be 10% under-leveled, necessitating engagement with the crafting system to bridge the power gap.
*   **Skill Tree:** The tree is balanced around "Specialization vs. Generalization." Early nodes provide flat bonuses (easier for casuals), while late-game nodes provide percentage-based bonuses (scaling for hardcore players).
*   **Time-to-Max:** Estimated at 120 hours of active play. This fits the "Linear Strategy" profile, providing a definitive "end" while leaving room for DLC/Expansion tiers.

### 3. Loot Balance
**Analysis:**
*   **Drop Rates:** Common materials have an 80% drop rate from debris, while "Void Shards" (Rare) sit at 5%. 
*   **Pity System:** To prevent "RNG Rage," a **Pseudo-Random Distribution** is implemented. For every 10 asteroids mined without a Void Shard, the drop chance increases by 2% until one drops, then resets.
*   **Reward Scaling:** Loot quality scales with Sector Difficulty. Farming Level 1 zones at Level 20 yields a 90% reduction in Exo-Data gains, forcing players to move forward in the linear progression.

### 4. Monetization Balance
**Analysis:**
*   **Pay-to-Win Risk:** Low. Since there is no P2P trading or PvP, "buying power" only accelerates the player through the linear story faster. It does not grant an unfair advantage over others.
*   **Value Proposition:** The primary monetization is "Time-Savers" (Plasma Core refills) and "Cosmetics" (Ship Skins). 
*   **Price Points:** Micro-transactions are tiered ($4.99 to $49.99). The "Starter Pack" provides a permanent +10% storage capacity, which is the highest value-per-dollar item to convert free players to paid.
*   **Power Balance:** Premium items are limited to "Side-grades." A paid ship might have more cargo space but less armor than a crafted ship of the same tier.

### 5. Engagement Balance
**Analysis:**
*   **Daily Commitment:** Optimal play requires ~45 minutes daily to clear "Daily Trade Routes." This is low enough to prevent burnout but high enough to form a habit.
*   **FOMO Risk:** Moderate. "Limited Time Trade Contracts" appear daily. To mitigate stress, players can "stack" up to 3 days of missed contracts.
*   **Casual vs. Hardcore:** Casuals can progress through the story using "Trade Bonds" to buy basic gear. Hardcore players must engage with the "Void Shard" crafting system to obtain "Elite" tier gear required for optional end-game "Void Rifts."

### 6. Overall Recommendations

**Critical Balance Issues:**
*   **The "Credit Glut":** Late-game players may accumulate too many Trade Bonds with nothing to buy once their ship is maxed.
*   **Crafting Bottleneck:** The 5% drop rate for Void Shards may feel too restrictive during the mid-game (Levels 15-25), potentially causing player churn.

**Suggested Adjustments:**
1.  **Introduce a "Luxury Sink":** Add high-cost cosmetic base decorations or "Fleet Titles" that cost millions of Trade Bonds to drain the late-game economy.
2.  **Shard Transmutation:** Allow players to break down 50 Common Materials into 1 Void Shard to provide a deterministic path for unlucky players.

**Testing Priorities:**
*   **Economy Stress Test:** Simulate 1,000 hours of play to see if Trade Bonds inflate beyond 1,000% of the starting value.
*   **Churn Point Analysis:** Closely monitor player retention at Level 15 (the first major crafting gate).

**Monitoring Metrics:**
*   **DARPU (Daily Average Revenue Per User).**
*   **Resource Velocity:** How fast are Plasma Cores being spent vs. regenerated?
*   **Gini Coefficient:** Measure the wealth gap between players to ensure the "NPC Economy" is pricing items fairly for all segments.
