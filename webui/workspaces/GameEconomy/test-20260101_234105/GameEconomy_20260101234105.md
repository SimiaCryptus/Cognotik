# Game Economy Design: Galactic Trader

**Started:** 2026-01-01 23:41:05
## Game Economy Design

**Game:** Galactic Trader

**Type:** strategy

**Progression Style:** linear

## Resource System Design

In **Galactic Trader**, despite the title, the "trading" aspect is abstracted into a linear progression system where players fulfill automated contracts through crafting and resource management rather than an open-market exchange. The economy is a "closed-loop" system designed to drive the player through increasingly complex tiers of space exploration.

---

### 1. Resource Identity: **Asteroid Ore (Material)**
*   **Type**: Common Material
*   **Purpose**: The fundamental building block for all physical assets. It represents the raw mass required to build ship hulls, station modules, and basic components.
*   **Thematic Fit**: As a space strategy game, the extraction of raw minerals from asteroid belts is the primary "input" for the industrial machine.

### 2. Resource Identity: **Galactic Credits (Currency)**
*   **Type**: Standard Currency
*   **Purpose**: The "lubricant" of the economy. Credits are required to pay for the assembly process (crafting fees), crew wages, and facility maintenance.
*   **Thematic Fit**: Represents the economic power and liquidity the player gains by fulfilling interstellar delivery contracts.

### 3. Resource Identity: **Research Data (Experience)**
*   **Type**: Progression/Experience
*   **Purpose**: Acts as the gatekeeper for linear progression. It is spent to unlock new blueprints, larger ship classes, and access to higher-level star systems.
*   **Thematic Fit**: Represents the knowledge gained from exploring the unknown and analyzing celestial phenomena.

### 4. Resource Identity: **Void Shards (Premium/Rare)**
*   **Type**: Premium / Rare Material
*   **Purpose**: Used for "Overclocking" (speeding up crafting), purchasing elite cosmetic ship skins, or crafting "Tier 0" experimental technology that bypasses standard limitations.
*   **Thematic Fit**: A mysterious, non-baryonic substance found only in the hearts of collapsed stars or through high-level achievements.

---

### Generation Sources

 Resource | Primary Source | Amount | Frequency | Scaling |
 :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
 **Ore** | Automated Mining Drones | 100–500 per cycle | Constant (Passive) | Increases with Drill Upgrades |
 **Credits** | Contract Fulfillment | 1,000–50,000 | Per Delivery | Increases with Ship Cargo Capacity |
 **Data** | Planetary Scanning | 10–50 per scan | Active (Exploration) | Increases with Sensor Tech |
 **Shards** | Rare Anomalies / IAPs | 1–5 per event | Very Rare | Fixed (Maintains scarcity) |

---

### Consumption Uses

*   **Asteroid Ore**: 
    *   *Use*: Crafting "Trade Goods" (e.g., Hull Plates, Engine Coils).
    *   *Cost*: 50–5,000 units per craft.
    *   *Value*: Necessary to create the items required to fulfill contracts and earn Credits.
*   **Galactic Credits**:
    *   *Use*: Upgrading the "Assembly Line" and "Hangar."
    *   *Cost*: 5,000–1,000,000+ Credits.
    *   *Value*: Increases the efficiency and throughput of the player’s operation.
*   **Research Data**:
    *   *Use*: Unlocking the next "Tech Tier" (Linear Progression).
    *   *Cost*: Exponentially increasing (100, 500, 2500, etc.).
    *   *Value*: Allows the player to craft higher-value goods and move to more profitable sectors.
*   **Void Shards**:
    *   *Use*: Instant-completion of long craft times or "Relic" ship parts.
    *   *Cost*: 1–50 Shards.
    *   *Value*: Saves time and provides a power spike for difficult late-game sectors.

---

### Storage and Limits

*   **Ore**: Limited by **Cargo Bay Capacity**. Players must upgrade their warehouse to hold more. This prevents infinite hoarding and forces players to engage with the crafting system.
*   **Credits**: **Unlimited**. As a digital currency, there is no thematic reason to cap it, though inflation is managed via sinks.
*   **Data**: Limited by **Mainframe Memory**. Players must "spend" data on research to clear space for new scans, ensuring they don't skip progression tiers.
*   **Void Shards**: **Unlimited**. Being a premium-adjacent resource, capping it would frustrate players.

---

### Exchange Rates (Internal Conversion)

Since "Trading" (Player-to-Player or Market) is disabled, the economy relies on **Fixed Conversion Crafting**:
*   **The Crafting Loop**: 500 Ore + 1,000 Credits $\rightarrow$ 1 "Advanced Circuit."
*   **The Progression Loop**: 10 "Advanced Circuits" + 500 Data $\rightarrow$ "Tier 2 License."
*   **The Premium Loop**: 1 Void Shard can be "shattered" into 10,000 Credits in emergencies, but Credits cannot be turned into Shards.

---

### Sink Mechanisms

1.  **Maintenance Fees**: Every ship in the player's fleet consumes a small amount of **Credits** per minute. This prevents the player from idling indefinitely to accumulate infinite wealth.
2.  **Refining Loss**: Converting Raw Ore into "Pure Ingots" (a crafting sub-step) results in a 10% loss of material, acting as a permanent **Ore** sink.
3.  **Data Decay**: If the player’s Mainframe is full, any new **Data** acquired is lost. This encourages constant spending on the tech tree.
4.  **The "Prestige" Ship**: A late-game ship that requires astronomical amounts of **Credits** and **Ore** to build, serving as a long-term goal for veteran players to dump excess resources.

---

### Design Philosophy Summary
This system creates a **Linear Dependency**: 
*   You need **Ore** to make goods. 
*   You need **Credits** to fund the making of those goods. 
*   Fulfilling goods contracts gives you the **Data** and **Credits** needed to reach the next tier. 
*   **Void Shards** provide the "shortcut" for players who want to progress faster than the linear curve allows. 

By removing open trading, the game becomes a puzzle of **Efficiency and Optimization**, where the player must balance their mining rate against their crafting speed to ensure their "Galactic Trade" empire never stalls.

## Progression System Design

This design for **Galactic Trader** focuses on a "Production-to-Contract" loop. Since player-to-player or NPC-market trading is disabled, the "Trading" aspect is reimagined as **Contract Fulfillment**: players harvest raw materials, craft them into high-value goods, and "trade" them to the Galactic Federation to earn progression and currency.

---

### Part 1: Resource System Design

The economy relies on four distinct resources that create a "Harvest → Refine → Assemble → Profit" flow.

 Resource | Identity | Generation | Consumption |
 :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
 **1. Raw Ore** (Material) | The foundational building block. Bulky and low value. | Mining asteroids. Yields 50-200 per node. Scales with Drill Tier. | Used in all Tier 1 crafting and as a base for refined alloys. |
 **2. Isotope Fuel** (Energy) | The "Action" resource. Limits how much you can travel/mine. | Siphoning gas giants or solar winds. 10 units per minute. | Spent on travel between sectors and powering crafting stations. |
 **3. Tech Fragments** (Experience) | Data-driven resource used for "Researching" (Leveling). | Scanning anomalies or salvaging shipwrecks. 5-10 per scan. | Spent at the Research Lab to convert into XP and unlock Blueprints. |
 **4. Credits** (Currency) | The reward for "Trading" (Contract fulfillment). | Completing delivery contracts. Rewards scale with item complexity. | Buying ship upgrades, expanding cargo holds, and unlocking new sectors. |

**Storage & Sinks:**
*   **Storage:** Ore and Fuel require physical Cargo Bays. Credits and Tech Fragments are digital (unlimited).
*   **Sinks:** Ship maintenance (Credits), Fuel consumption (Isotope), and "Experimental Crafting" (Ore/Tech) where resources are consumed for a chance at high-tier modules.

---

### Part 2: Progression System (30 Levels)

The progression is **Linear**. Each level requires a set amount of XP, increasing by a flat increment to ensure the endgame remains reachable for casual players while providing a steady rhythm for hardcore players.

**XP Formula:** $XP\ Required = 1,000 + (Level \times 500)$
*   *Level 1:* 1,500 XP
*   *Level 30:* 16,000 XP
*   *Total XP to Max:* ~262,500 XP

#### Unlock Schedule & Milestones

 Tier | Levels | Focus | Key Unlocks |
 :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
 **I: Scout** | 1-5 | Basics | Level 2: Basic Alloy Crafting; Level 5: **Milestone: First Warp Drive** |
 **II: Miner** | 6-10 | Extraction | Level 7: Automated Mining Drones; Level 10: **Milestone: Medium Cargo Ship** |
 **III: Refiner** | 11-15 | Efficiency | Level 12: Advanced Smeltery; Level 15: **Milestone: Sector 2 Access** |
 **IV: Merchant** | 16-20 | Complexity | Level 18: Multi-stage Assembly; Level 20: **Milestone: Heavy Freighter** |
 **V: Tycoon** | 21-25 | Automation | Level 22: Orbital Logistics Hub; Level 25: **Milestone: Sector 3 (Deep Space)** |
 **VI: Galactic Icon** | 26-30 | Mastery | Level 28: Exotic Matter Synthesis; Level 30: **Milestone: The Mothership** |

**Estimated Time to Max Level:** 45–60 Hours.
*   **Casual (5 hrs/week):** ~3 months.
*   **Hardcore (20 hrs/week):** ~2-3 weeks.

---

### Part 3: Skill Trees

Players earn **1 Skill Point per level** (29 total). There are three branches, allowing players to specialize in how they interact with the linear progression.

#### Branch 1: The Industrialist (Crafting & Efficiency)
*Focuses on getting more out of less.*
1.  **Material Thrift:** 5/10/15% chance to not consume Raw Ore when crafting.
2.  **Rapid Assembly:** Reduces crafting time by 20%.
3.  **Master Artisan:** (Synergy) Crafted items have a 10% chance to be "High Quality," yielding 50% more Credits on contracts.

#### Branch 2: The Pathfinder (Exploration & Tech)
*Focuses on Tech Fragment acquisition and Fuel efficiency.*
1.  **Deep Scanner:** Increases Tech Fragment find rate by 20%.
2.  **Ion Efficiency:** Reduces Isotope Fuel consumption during warp by 15%.
3.  **Data Miner:** (Synergy) Converting Tech Fragments into XP grants a 5% bonus to Credits earned for the next hour.

#### Branch 3: The Logistics Expert (Storage & Scale)
*Focuses on inventory management and contract rewards.*
1.  **Compact Packing:** Increases Cargo Bay capacity by 25%.
2.  **Contract Negotiator:** Increases Credit rewards from all contracts by 10%.
3.  **Bulk Trader:** (Synergy) Completing 3 contracts within 10 minutes grants a massive XP burst.

---

### Part 4: Mechanics & Balance

**Respec Mechanics:**
Players can "Re-calibrate" their ship's AI at any Space Station.
*   **Cost:** 25% of current Credit balance (ensures it's always affordable but feels like a significant decision).
*   **Frequency:** Unlimited, but the cost prevents constant switching between "Mining mode" and "Crafting mode."

**Synergy Example:**
A player who builds into **Material Thrift** (Industrialist) and **Contract Negotiator** (Logistics) creates a "High-Margin" strategy. They spend very few resources to create goods and sell them for maximum profit, allowing them to bypass heavy mining grinds in the late game.

**Inflation Prevention:**
As the player reaches Level 30, the "Mothership" milestone introduces **Resource Sinks**. The Mothership requires a weekly "Maintenance Tithe" of Raw Ore and Isotope Fuel to remain operational. This prevents the player from hoarding infinite resources and keeps the core gameplay loop (mining and harvesting) relevant even at max level.

## Loot & Reward System Design

This design builds upon the **Galactic Trader** economy, focusing on a linear progression strategy where players move through increasingly difficult sectors. Since trading is disabled, the "Trader" aspect is thematic—players "trade" their time and tactical prowess for resources used in the robust crafting system.

---

### 1. Resource Recap
To ensure the loot system is grounded, we utilize these four resources:
1.  **Galactic Credits (Currency):** Used for ship repairs and basic component purchases.
2.  **Raw Ore (Material):** The bulk material for hull upgrades and basic crafting.
3.  **Energy Cells (Fuel/Material):** Required for sector jumps and high-tier tech crafting.
4.  **Tech Fragments (XP/Gated Material):** Used to unlock new tiers of the tech tree.

---

### 2. Loot Tables & Content Types

#### A. Common Activities (Asteroid Mining & Scout Drones)
*Focus: High-frequency, low-intensity resource gathering.*
*   **Item Types & Rarities:**
    *   **Common (85%):** Raw Ore (Small stacks), Galactic Credits (Small amounts).
    *   **Rare (14%):** Energy Cells (Single units), Refined Ore.
    *   **Epic (1%):** Damaged Tech Fragment.
*   **Scaling:** Resource quantity increases by 15% per sector level.
*   **Pity System:** "Deep Scan" – Every 10th asteroid/scout is guaranteed to drop a Rare tier reward.

#### B. Elite Enemies (Mercenary Cruisers & Anomalies)
*Focus: Mid-tier challenges requiring tactical play.*
*   **Item Types & Rarities:**
    *   **Common (50%):** Raw Ore (Large stacks), Galactic Credits (Medium amounts).
    *   **Rare (40%):** Energy Cells (3-5 units), Weapon Components.
    *   **Epic (10%):** Tech Fragments (1-2 units).
*   **Scaling:** Elites in higher sectors have a higher weight for Tech Fragments (up to 20%).
*   **Pity System:** "Salvage Rights" – If an Elite does not drop a Tech Fragment, the drop rate for the next Elite increases by 5% (stacks until drop).

#### C. Boss Encounters (Sector Guardians & Motherships)
*Focus: Major progression milestones.*
*   **Item Types & Rarities:**
    *   **Rare (Guaranteed):** Massive Credit Cache, Full Energy Recharge.
    *   **Epic (Guaranteed):** 5x Tech Fragments, 1x Advanced Blueprint (Linear progression item).
    *   **Legendary (10%):** "Prototype Component" (Unique ship part with special abilities).
*   **Scaling:** Bosses drop "Sector Keys" required to move to the next linear progression zone.
*   **Pity System:** "Research Data" – Defeating a boss always grants a "Boss Token." 3 Tokens can be crafted into a Legendary Prototype Component of the player's choice.

---

### 3. Reward Structures

#### Quest Completion (The Narrative Path)
Quests are the primary driver of the linear progression.
*   **Primary Quests:** Reward **Tech Fragments** and **Blueprints**. These are non-random to ensure the player never gets "stuck" behind RNG for essential upgrades.
*   **Side Quests:** Reward **Energy Cells** and **Credits**. These allow players to "over-level" their ship's endurance and economy before moving to the next sector.
*   **Scaling:** Quest rewards are fixed based on the Sector Tier to prevent low-level farming from being viable in the late game.

#### Achievement Unlocks (The Completionist Path)
Achievements provide permanent, passive buffs rather than raw resources.
*   **"Master Miner" (Mine 1,000 Asteroids):** +10% Raw Ore yield permanently.
*   **"Ace Pilot" (Destroy 500 Pirates):** -5% Credit cost for ship repairs.
*   **"Tech Specialist" (Craft 50 Items):** 5% chance to not consume Energy Cells when crafting.

#### Milestone Rewards (The Growth Path)
Triggered when the player reaches specific power levels or ship tiers.
*   **Tier 1 (The Voyager):** Reaching Sector 3 grants a "Cargo Expansion," increasing Raw Ore storage capacity by 50%.
*   **Tier 2 (The Enforcer):** Reaching Sector 6 grants a "Reactor Overclock," increasing the maximum Energy Cell cap.
*   **Tier 3 (The Galactic Legend):** Reaching the final sector grants the "Infinite Jump Drive" blueprint.

#### First-Time Bonuses
To encourage exploration of new mechanics:
*   **First Craft:** Returns 100% of materials used.
*   **First Elite Kill:** Guaranteed Epic Tech Fragment.
*   **First Sector Jump:** Bonus 500 Galactic Credits to kickstart the new area's economy.

---

### 4. System Balance & Logic

#### Avoiding Randomness Frustration
The system uses a **"Hybrid Loot Model."** While common drops are randomized to keep the gameplay loop engaging, all *progression-critical* items (Blueprints and Tech Fragments) have high-floor pity systems or are guaranteed via Quests. This ensures a player's forward momentum is never halted by a "bad streak" of luck.

#### Upgrade Paths
1.  **Early Game:** Focus on Raw Ore and Credits to build a sturdy hull.
2.  **Mid Game:** Focus on Energy Cells and Tech Fragments to enable longer sorties and better weapons.
3.  **Late Game:** Focus on Legendary Prototype Components and Boss Tokens to optimize the ship for the final encounter.

#### Inflation Prevention (Sinks)
*   **Repair Costs:** Scale with ship tier, ensuring Galactic Credits remain relevant.
*   **Energy Consumption:** Higher-tier weapons and engines consume more Energy Cells, requiring constant replenishment through gameplay.
*   **Tech Fragment Refinement:** Excess Tech Fragments can be "burned" to provide a temporary 10% boost to combat stats, preventing them from piling up once the tech tree is maxed.

## Monetization Strategy Design

To design a monetization strategy for **Galactic Trader**, we must first briefly define the four-resource economy that supports it. Since player-to-player trading is disabled, the economy is a "closed loop" between the player and the game environment, which makes monetization easier to balance without risking a market collapse.

### The Core Economy (Quick Reference)
1.  **Credits (Currency):** Earned via missions; used for fuel, repairs, and NPC bribes.
2.  **Alloy Scraps (Material):** Salvaged from wrecks; used for crafting ship components.
3.  **Data Cores (Progression):** Found in anomalies; used to unlock the linear Tech Tree.
4.  **Nova Crystals (Premium):** Purchased with real money; used for cosmetics and convenience.

---

### 1. Optional Purchases (The "Nova Store")
The store focuses on removing friction and providing aesthetic flair rather than selling power.

*   **Convenience Items:**
    *   **Cargo Expansion Modules ($4.99):** Permanently increases the storage limit for Alloy Scraps and Data Cores. *Value: Reduces the need for frequent inventory management.*
    *   **Automated Salvage Drones ($1.99 for 24h):** Automatically collects Alloy Scraps from nearby debris fields while the player is flying. *Value: Passive resource gain for busy players.*
    *   **Warp Jump Refills ($0.99):** Instantly refills the ship's fuel (Credits can also do this, but this is a "quick fix").

*   **Time Savers:**
    *   **Research Accelerators ($2.99):** Reduces the "cooldown" or "analysis time" required when spending Data Cores on the Tech Tree by 50% for 4 hours.
    *   **Resource Doubler (7 Days) ($9.99):** Doubles the Alloy Scraps found during exploration.

*   **Price Points:**
    *   **Micro-transactions:** $0.99 – $4.99 (Consumables/Refills)
    *   **Mid-tier:** $9.99 – $19.99 (Bundles/Skins)
    *   **Whale-tier:** $49.99 – $99.99 (Bulk Nova Crystals with 20% bonus)

---

### 2. Battle Pass: "The Galactic Ledger"
The Ledger provides a seasonal progression track (60 days) that encourages daily play.

*   **Price:** $9.99 (Premium Track) / $19.99 (Premium + 20 Level Skip).
*   **Free Tier Rewards:** Small amounts of Credits, basic Alloy Scraps, and one "Rare" ship decal at Level 50.
*   **Premium Tier Rewards:**
    *   Exclusive Ship Hulls (Cosmetic).
    *   Nova Crystal rebates (earning enough to buy the next pass).
    *   Unique "Bridge Pets" (animated NPCs for the ship interior).
    *   High-tier crafting recipes (aesthetic variants of standard gear).
*   **Completion Time:** Estimated 40–50 hours of gameplay over 2 months.
*   **Value Proposition:** Provides roughly $50 worth of items and currency for $10, rewarding player retention.

---

### 3. Cosmetics
Cosmetics are the primary revenue driver, allowing players to express their identity in the galaxy.

*   **Categories:**
    *   **Ship Hulls (Skins):** Total model swaps or high-quality textures (e.g., "Void-Reaper" or "Federation Gold").
    *   **Engine Trails:** Changes the color and effect of the ship's exhaust (e.g., Blue Ion, Green Plasma, Rainbow).
    *   **Bridge Interiors:** Customizable furniture and lighting for the player's cockpit.
    *   **UI Themes:** Custom HUD colors and sound effects.

*   **Rarity Tiers:**
    *   **Common (White):** Simple color swaps ($1.99).
    *   **Rare (Blue):** Patterned skins and basic engine glows ($4.99).
    *   **Epic (Purple):** Animated textures and unique UI themes ($9.99).
    *   **Legendary (Gold):** Full model changes with unique sound effects ($19.99).

*   **Acquisition:** Direct purchase via the Nova Store or earned through the Galactic Ledger.

---

### 4. Pay-to-Win (P2W) Risk Assessment

 Purchase Type | P2W Risk | Analysis & Safeguards |
 :--- | :--- | :--- |
 **Resource Doublers** | Medium | **Risk:** Players could bypass the linear progression too quickly. **Safeguard:** Doublers only affect *Materials*, not *Data Cores* (Progression). Players still have to play the game to find the anomalies. |
 **Cargo Expansion** | Low | **Risk:** Inventory management is a core strategy element. **Safeguard:** Cap the maximum number of purchasable slots so that "infinite" storage isn't possible, maintaining the need for strategic resource choice. |
 **Warp Refills** | Low | **Risk:** Skipping the Credit-grind for fuel. **Safeguard:** Fuel is cheap and abundant via gameplay; the purchase is purely for extreme convenience during long sessions. |
 **Battle Pass Gear** | Low | **Risk:** Exclusive gear being stronger than base gear. **Safeguard:** All Battle Pass equipment must be "Side-grades" or purely cosmetic variants of items already unlockable in the Tech Tree. |

### Final Recommendation:
To maintain the "Linear Progression" feel of *Galactic Trader*, the monetization must never allow a player to "Buy a Level." By keeping **Data Cores** (the progression resource) strictly non-purchasable, the game ensures that even the highest-spending players must actually explore the galaxy and engage with the strategy mechanics to advance. This preserves the integrity of the game's challenge while allowing for a robust, profitable cosmetic economy.

## Engagement Systems Design

This engagement system is designed for **Galactic Trader**, a linear strategy game focused on resource management and crafting. Since player-to-player trading is disabled, these systems focus on the relationship between the player, the NPC factions, and the progression curve.

---

### 1. Daily Rewards: The "Logistics Check-in"
To encourage daily logins, the reward system mimics a "Daily Supply Drop" from the Galactic Command.

*   **Reward Structure (7-Day Cycle):**
    *   **Days 1-2:** Small bundles of **Raw Ore** (Material) to kickstart crafting.
    *   **Days 3-4:** Moderate amounts of **Credits** (Currency) for ship maintenance.
    *   **Day 5:** A "Tech Cache" containing **Tech Fragments** (Progression).
    *   **Day 6:** Rare crafting components or a small amount of **Antimatter** (Premium).
    *   **Day 7:** "The Grand Shipment"—A large bundle of all four resources plus a temporary 24-hour production buff.
*   **Streak Bonuses:** Completing a full 7-day streak grants a "Loyalty Multiplier" (+5%) to all resource generation for the following week, stacking up to 15%.
*   **Catch-up Mechanics:** Players can spend a small amount of **Credits** to "Recover" a missed day, ensuring the streak isn't lost due to real-life interruptions.
*   **Value Scaling:** Reward amounts are not fixed; they are calculated as `Base Value * (1 + Player Level/10)`. This ensures rewards remain relevant as the player moves from a small shuttle to a massive freighter.

---

### 2. Seasonal Content: "Galactic Eras"
Seasons provide a long-term macro-goal to prevent the linear progression from feeling stagnant.

*   **Season Duration:** 60 Days (6 seasons per year).
*   **Seasonal Themes:** Themes rotate between "Industrial Revolution" (focus on mining/crafting), "The Great Expansion" (focus on exploration/tech), and "Economic Boom" (focus on credit accumulation).
*   **Exclusive Rewards:** 
    *   **Blueprints:** Unique ship hulls or modules only craftable during the season.
    *   **Cosmetics:** Ship decals and engine trail colors.
    *   **Legacy Perks:** Small permanent buffs (e.g., +2% cargo capacity) that carry over to the permanent profile.
*   **Seasonal Progression:** A "Season Pass" style track progressed by earning "Trade XP." Trade XP is gained naturally through the core loop: Mining Ore -> Crafting Goods -> Selling for Credits.
*   **Retention Impact:** High. It provides a "reason to play now" (FOMO) while rewarding the core gameplay loop.

---

### 3. Events: "Sector Anomalies"
Events break the routine of the linear progression with high-intensity, short-term goals.

*   **Event Types:**
    *   **Asteroid Pulse (Recurring - Weekends):** Asteroid fields yield 2x **Raw Ore**. Encourages heavy crafting sessions.
    *   **Tech Breakthrough (Limited-Time - 3 Days):** The cost of **Tech Fragments** for upgrades is reduced by 25%.
    *   **The Gilded Merchant (Special - 24 Hours):** An NPC trader appears who buys crafted goods for 1.5x **Credits**.
*   **Event Frequency:** One minor event every weekend; one major "Sector Anomaly" once per month.
*   **Participation Incentives:** Milestone rewards. "Craft 50 Warp Coils during the event to receive a Prototype Engine Blueprint."
*   **Balance:** Events are designed to be "Resource Sinks" or "Accelerators." For example, a 2x Ore event is followed by a "High Demand" event to drain the excess inventory from the economy.

---

### 4. Retention Mechanics: "The Commander’s Legacy"
These systems focus on long-term player investment and social proof.

*   **Login Bonuses (The "Welcome Back" Gift):**
    *   If a player is away for >72 hours, they receive a "Rested Production" bonus. Their mines/refineries operate at 150% speed for the first 2 hours of their return to help them catch up to the linear curve.
*   **Social Features (Galactic Guilds):**
    *   While there is no player trading, players can join **Guilds** to contribute resources toward "Mega-Structures" (e.g., a Guild Space Station). 
    *   **Guild Goals:** "Collect 1,000,000 Ore as a group." Completing these grants all members a passive "Guild Tax Break" (reduced crafting costs).
*   **Comeback Rewards:** A "Veteran’s Re-entry Kit" for players returning after 30+ days, providing enough **Tech Fragments** to instantly skip one minor progression tier, preventing them from feeling "behind the curve."
*   **Expected Retention Impact:** 
    *   *Short-term:* Daily rewards ensure Day 1 to Day 7 retention.
    *   *Mid-term:* Events and Guild Goals drive weekly engagement.
    *   *Long-term:* Seasonal rewards and the "Mega-Structure" progression keep players invested for 6+ months.

---

### Summary Table: Engagement Impact

 System | Frequency | Primary Reward | Retention Goal |
 :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
 **Daily Rewards** | Daily | Mixed Resources | DAU (Daily Active Users) |
 **Seasonal Content** | 60 Days | Blueprints/Skins | MAU (Monthly Active Users) |
 **Events** | Weekly | Resource Multipliers | Session Length / Intensity |
 **Guild Goals** | Continuous | Passive Buffs | Community / Social Pressure |

This system ensures that **Galactic Trader** feels like a living universe where the player's daily actions contribute to both their personal linear growth and a larger, community-driven narrative.

This design for **Galactic Trader** focuses on a tightly controlled, linear progression loop where players "trade" with the game environment (NPC stations and factions) rather than other players. This ensures the developer maintains total control over inflation and progression pacing.

# Part 1: Resource System Design

### 1. Resource Identity

 Resource | Type | Purpose | Thematic Fit |
 :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
 **Galactic Credits (GC)** | Currency | Standard medium of exchange for NPC services, repairs, and basic parts. | The universal currency of the United Star Systems. |
 **Raw Isotope** | Material | Primary input for crafting ship components and base modules. | Unrefined minerals mined from asteroids and planets. |
 **Data Cores** | Progression | Used to unlock new tiers in the Tech Tree (Research). | Encrypted blueprints and ancient alien schematics. |
 **Void Matter** | Rare/Premium | Bottleneck resource for endgame gear and "Instant-Finish" crafting. | Highly volatile energy found only in deep-space anomalies. |

### 2. Generation Sources
*   **GC:** Earned via mission completion (500–5,000 GC) and selling refined goods to NPC stations. Scaling: +15% per sector level.
*   **Raw Isotope:** Acquired via mining lasers. Yields 50–200 units per asteroid. Frequency: Constant during flight.
*   **Data Cores:** Found in derelict ship salvaging or as rare mission rewards. Frequency: Low (1–3 per hour).
*   **Void Matter:** Daily login rewards, high-level boss encounters, or premium purchase. Frequency: Very Low (Fixed daily cap).

### 3. Consumption Uses
*   **GC:** Ship repairs (Frequent, low cost), Crew salaries (Passive drain), Buying basic components.
*   **Raw Isotope:** Crafting (e.g., 500 for a Hull Plate). High volume, high frequency.
*   **Data Cores:** Researching Tier 2–5 tech. Costs scale exponentially (10, 50, 250, 1250).
*   **Void Matter:** Crafting "Legendary" tier items or bypassing 24-hour craft timers.

### 4. Storage and Limits
*   **GC/Data Cores:** Unlimited digital storage.
*   **Raw Isotope:** Limited by Ship Cargo Hold. Requires "Silo" base buildings to store large quantities.
*   **Void Matter:** Limited by "Containment Field" level to prevent hoarding.
*   **Overflow:** Excess isotopes are automatically vented (lost) unless the player upgrades storage, driving the "Storage Upgrade" loop.

### 5. Exchange Rates (NPC Only)
*   **Refining:** 5 Raw Isotopes → 1 Refined Alloy (Value increases by 3x).
*   **Liquidation:** 1 Refined Alloy → 50 GC.
*   **Black Market:** 10,000 GC → 1 Data Core (Limited weekly stock to prevent credit bloating).

### 6. Sink Mechanisms
*   **Maintenance:** Ships take "wear and tear" damage over time, requiring GC.
*   **Refining Loss:** 10% of Raw Isotopes are lost during the refining process (adjustable for balance).
*   **Expansions:** High GC costs for unlocking new hangar bays and larger ships.

## Balance Report

This document outlines the economic architecture and balance analysis for **Galactic Trader**, a linear strategy game focused on ship progression and sector mastery.

# Part 1: Resource System Design

### 1. Resource Identity
 Resource | Type | Role | Thematic Fit |
 :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
 **Scrap Metal** | Material | Basic building block for all crafting. | Salvaged from derelict ships and asteroids. |
 **Plasma Cells** | Material/Fuel | High-energy catalyst for advanced tech and travel. | Harvested from gas giants and star coronas. |
 **Galactic Credits** | Currency | Medium of exchange for system services. | Standardized digital currency of the Union. |
 **Neural Data** | Experience | Unlocks new blueprints and skill tiers. | Encrypted files recovered from ancient beacons. |

### 2. Generation Sources
*   **Scrap Metal:** High frequency. Acquired via mining (10-50 per node) and combat salvage. Scales +15% per sector level.
*   **Plasma Cells:** Medium frequency. Acquired via gas siphoning (5-20 per node). Scaling is gated by ship harvester quality.
*   **Galactic Credits:** Reward for mission completion (500–5,000). Fixed amounts based on mission difficulty.
*   **Neural Data:** Low frequency. Found in "Data Caches" at the end of sectors or rare boss drops.

### 3. Consumption Uses
*   **Crafting:** Scrap + Plasma = Ship Components (Engines, Shields, Hull).
*   **Upgrading:** Credits + Scrap = Leveling up existing components.
*   **Progression:** Neural Data is "spent" in the Research Lab to unlock the next tier of ship hulls.
*   **Maintenance:** Credits are spent on hull repairs and refueling.

### 4. Storage and Limits
*   **Storage:** Capped by "Cargo Bay" capacity.
*   **Rationale:** Forces players to make choices between hoarding materials for crafting or selling them for credits.
*   **Overflow:** Excess resources are automatically converted to a small amount of Credits (10% value) to prevent "wasted" gameplay feel.

### 5. Exchange Rates (System-Only)
*   **Scrap to Credits:** 1:5 (Sell price) / 1:15 (Buy price).
*   **Plasma to Credits:** 1:20 (Sell price) / 1:50 (Buy price).
*   **Neural Data:** Non-tradable (Progression Lock).

### 6. Sink Mechanisms
*   **Repair Costs:** Scaling credit sink that increases with ship size.
*   **Consumables:** Crafting one-time-use "Boosters" (Shield Overcharge) consumes Scrap and Plasma.
*   **Sector Taxes:** A flat Credit fee to jump to higher-tier sectors.

# Part 2: Comprehensive Balance Report

## 1. Resource Balance
*   **Generation vs. Consumption:** The current model shows a **Scrap Surplus** in early-game (Levels 1-5). To counter this, the "Hull Repair" mechanic must scale aggressively. Plasma Cells act as the primary bottleneck for mid-game progression, ensuring players cannot skip tiers.
*   **Storage Limits:** The Cargo Bay limit is the primary strategic friction point. It is balanced to hold 150% of the resources required for a single major upgrade, forcing a "Gather -> Craft -> Repeat" loop.
*   **Sink Effectiveness:** The "Sector Tax" effectively prevents Credit inflation by removing 20% of a player's liquid wealth upon entering a new tier, ensuring they start the next "chapter" with a need to engage with the economy.

## 2. Progression Balance
*   **XP (Neural Data) Curve:** Progression is **Polynomial ($y = x^{1.5}$)**. This ensures the first 5 levels feel rapid (hooking the player), while levels 15-20 require significant exploration.
*   **Unlock Pacing:** Blueprints are spaced every 2 levels. This creates a "Power Spike" followed by a "Refinement Period" where the player upgrades that specific gear.
*   **Time-to-Max:** Estimated at 30 hours for a standard playthrough. The linear path is gated by "Boss Beacons" that require specific Plasma-based tech to defeat.

## 3. Loot Balance
*   **Drop Rates:** 
    *   Common (Scrap): 80%
    *   Rare (Plasma): 15%
    *   Epic (Neural Data): 5%
*   **Pity System:** If a player clears 5 asteroid fields without a Plasma Cell drop, the 6th field has a 100% guaranteed drop rate. This prevents "RNG-screwed" progression stalls.

## 4. Monetization Balance (Premium Strategy)
*   **Pay-to-Win Risk:** **Low.** Since there is no P2P trading or PvP, "buying" resources only accelerates the linear path. 
*   **Value Proposition:** Monetization focuses on "Permanent Utility" (e.g., +20% Cargo Capacity) rather than consumable resources.
*   **Cosmetic vs. Power:** 70% of revenue is projected from Ship Skins and Engine Trail effects. Power-ups are limited to "Time Savers" (2x Scrap for 1 hour).

## 5. Engagement Balance
*   **Daily Commitment:** Designed for a "Burst" playstyle. A 20-minute session allows for 2-3 mission completions and 1 ship upgrade.
*   **Burnout Prevention:** The linear sector transitions provide natural "stopping points." Once a sector is cleared, the player feels a sense of completion, reducing the "endless grind" fatigue.
*   **Casual vs. Hardcore:** Casuals can progress by over-leveling in safe sectors; Hardcore players can "speedrun" by tackling high-level sectors with low-tier gear, rewarded by higher Credit multipliers.

## 6. Overall Recommendations
*   **Critical Issue:** Neural Data scarcity might lead to players feeling "stuck" if they lose a boss fight. 
*   **Adjustment:** Implement a "Data Recovery" mission that spawns if a player fails a boss fight twice, allowing them to grind a small amount of Neural Data to bridge the power gap.
*   **Testing Priority:** Monitor the **Plasma Cell bottleneck** at Level 10. If players spend more than 3 hours in Sector 2 without upgrading, the drop rate should be buffed by 5%.
*   **Monitoring Metrics:** 
    *   *DARPU (Daily Average Revenue Per User)*
    *   *Churn rate at Sector Transitions*
    *   *Resource Hoarding Ratio (Credits vs. Materials)*
