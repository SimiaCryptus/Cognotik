## Gameplay Mechanics

### Core Loop
The Resonance Cycle: A loop consisting of exploration and attunement to Fractures, engaging in combat or social challenges, making a Shard Decision (Order, Freedom, or Dominion), and experiencing a World Shift that alters physical laws and traversal.

### Key Systems
- Resonance Combat System: Alignment-based combat styles (Order, Freedom, Dominion) affecting magic and tactics.
- Living World Dialogue System: NPC reactions based on Ideological Pressure and the 'Shard’s Whisper' power options.
- Shard-Forging: A crafting system where gear is infused with Shard light to gain alignment-specific properties.

### Progression
The Shard-Link: A living skill tree with three branches (The Architect, The Catalyst, The Sovereign) featuring lock-out mechanics and stat evolution that physically transforms the player and the environment.

### Unique Mechanics
- The Laws of Eldoria: A system where the player's moral compass literally changes the game's physics engine.
- The Gravity Shift: Weakened gravity and increased verticality tied to the Freedom alignment.
- The Chronos Effect: Time manipulation and predictability mechanics tied to the Order alignment.
- The Entropy Tax: Resource scarcity and exponential power growth tied to the Dominion alignment.

### Ludonarrative Harmony
The Weight of Choice: Gameplay reinforces themes of Order vs. Freedom and the Corruption of Power by making Dominion an 'easy mode' with social isolation, Freedom a high-risk/unpredictable path, and Order a safe but stagnant experience.
