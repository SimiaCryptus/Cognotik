## Decision Points and Consequences

### 1. sunken_temple_shard

**Location:** The Sunken Temple

**Description:** The first Shard is found. It powers a village's water but is needed for the journey.

**Choices:**

#### Choice: Extract the Shard
- **Tone:** Pragmatic/Ruthless

**Consequences:**
- Gain immediate power/level up
- The village suffers

**Character Reactions:**
- **Kaelen:** +1
- **Lyra:** -1


#### Choice: Leave the Shard
- **Tone:** Altruistic

**Consequences:**
- The village thrives
- Party remains weak
- Must take a dangerous detour

**Character Reactions:**
- **Lyra:** +1
- **Kaelen:** -1

**Convergence Point:** Crossroads Camp
