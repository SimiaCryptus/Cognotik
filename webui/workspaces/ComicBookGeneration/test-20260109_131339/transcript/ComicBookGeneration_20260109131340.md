# The Silicon Bloom
*In the heart of Neo-Kowloon, a city of eternal rain and chrome, a discarded maintenance drone named "Unit 7" follows a leak in the cooling pipes to find something impossible: a living, breathing sanctuary of green hidden beneath the steel.*
## Characters
- **Unit 7 (Seven)**: An obsolete "Scrubber-Class" maintenance robot. He is built for utility, not aesthetics. (Boxy, rusted chassis with exposed wiring at the joints. He has a single, large circular optic sensor that glows a soft, flickering cyan. One of his hydraulic arms is slightly misaligned, giving him a limping gait.)
