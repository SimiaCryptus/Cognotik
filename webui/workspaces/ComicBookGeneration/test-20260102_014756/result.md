# Neon Chlorophyll
*In a rain-slicked megacity where "green" is just a color on a neon sign, an obsolete maintenance droid discovers a crack in the foundation of a skyscraper that leads to a forgotten, self-sustaining greenhouse.*
## Characters
- **UNIT 7-B ("Seven")**: A bulky, outdated sanitation and repair droid. He is built for utility, not aesthetics. (Boxy torso with rusted orange plating; a single, large circular optic sensor that glows a soft, flickering blue; hydraulic limbs that hiss with steam; various tools (wrenches, welders) integrated into his forearms.)
