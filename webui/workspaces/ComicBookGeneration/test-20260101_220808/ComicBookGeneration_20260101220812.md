# Comic Book Generation Task

# Neon Chlorophyll
*In a rain-slicked megacity where organic life has been replaced by synthetic substitutes, an obsolete maintenance droid named Unit 7-B discovers a hidden sanctuary of real nature beneath the steel floorboards of Sector 4.*
## Characters
- **Unit 7-B ("Seven")**: A heavy-duty, outdated maintenance robot designed for sewer and structural repair. He is slow-moving and contemplative for a machine. (Boxy, rusted chassis with patches of oxidized copper. He has a single, large circular optic sensor that glows a soft, flickering amber. One of his hydraulic arms is missing its casing, exposing rhythmic, pulsing pistons.)
