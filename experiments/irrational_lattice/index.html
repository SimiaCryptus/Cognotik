About

Each lattice site x ∈ ℤ² is assigned an algebraic displacement η(x) = a(x) + b(x)√D built from a sum of K incommensurate algebraic gratings. The visualization shows the magnitude of this deterministic, aperiodic field.

Lower D values and small α yield long-range moiré-like interference reminiscent of Penrose patterns, constrained to the square lattice.

Drag to pan and scroll to zoom into the rational lattice. Use Reset view to return to the origin.
