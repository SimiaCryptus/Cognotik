    About

    Centroids and vertices live in Q(√5)². Rotations by 72° are exact via cos(72°) = (√5−1)/4, sin(72°) = √(10+2√5)/4 (the radical is stored symbolically as S72 with S72² = (10+2√5)/16).

    For general n-gons, floating-point arithmetic is used with double precision. The Sierpiński triangle uses an IFS (iterated function system) with contraction ratio ½.
