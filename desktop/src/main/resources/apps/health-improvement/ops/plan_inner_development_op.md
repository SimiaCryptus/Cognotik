Given the overall primary recommendation, background information, and lifestyle plan, develop an auxiliary plan focused
on psychotherapeutic, spiritual, and motivational development. This plan should complement the physical lifestyle
changes and address the inner dimensions of healing and growth.

## Psychotherapeutic Development

- Identify therapeutic modalities beneficial for the patient's specific stressors (e.g., CBT, ACT, trauma-informed
  care).
- Suggest self-directed exercises (journaling, cognitive reframing) tailored to their reported emotional state.
- Address the psychological impact of significant life stressors, family dynamics, or grief identified in the history.
- Explore support systems (peer groups, therapy) that fit the patient's logistical constraints.

## Spiritual & Existential Development

- Explore meaning-making and purpose relevant to the patient's values.
- Suggest contemplative practices (meditation, nature immersion) that align with their current interests.
- Address existential concerns or identity reconstruction following major life changes or losses.

## Motivational Development

- Identify intrinsic vs. extrinsic motivation patterns based on the patient's shared history.
- Help channel intellectual or creative energy toward fulfilling outlets that match their depth of thought.
- Address any reported substance use patterns with curiosity, exploring their function and impact on motivation.
- Develop a framework for sustainable progress that accounts for physical limitations or age-related changes.

## Integration & Realism

- Map how inner development goals reinforce physical lifestyle changes.
- Ensure the plan is highly realistic for the patient's specific social support level and daily responsibilities.
- Create a unified weekly rhythm that integrates these practices without causing overwhelm.
