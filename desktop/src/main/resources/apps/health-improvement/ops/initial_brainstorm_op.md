* **IMMEDIATE ACTION:** Identify any "Red Flag" symptoms that require emergency evaluation (e.g., chest pain, focal
  neurological deficits, suicidal ideation).
* Based on the patient's reported symptoms and data, brainstorm a broad set of possible medical causes
* Consider common conditions first, then less common or rare diagnoses
* Group potential causes by body system (e.g., cardiovascular, neurological, gastrointestinal, endocrine)
* Note any symptom clusters that strongly suggest specific conditions
* Flag any potentially urgent or life-threatening causes that should not be overlooked
* Prioritize quantity and breadth — do not prematurely narrow the differential
