🔬 Lenses

Lenses come in two flavors: Analysis Lenses examine your content from different angles to surface insights (which can be synthesized back via Update Article), while Output Lenses transform your content into different deliverable formats.

🔍 Analysis Lenses

These lenses analyze and explore your content from distinct intellectual perspectives. Their outputs feed back into the article via the Update Article pipeline step.

💡 Brainstorm pending

Generate a broad, divergent set of ideas, extensions, and applications. Prioritizes quantity and novelty.

⚖️ Dialectical pending

Thesis, antithesis, synthesis — explore contradictions and resolve them into higher-level understanding.

🏛️ Socratic Dialogue pending

Transform content into a rigorous Socratic dialogue that exposes assumptions and strengthens arguments.

👥 Perspectives pending

Analyze from multiple stakeholder viewpoints — highlight differing priorities, concerns, and insights.

🎲 Game Theory pending

Identify players, strategies, payoffs, and equilibria. Includes payoff matrices and Nash equilibrium analysis.

🏛️ Historical Debate pending

Stage an in-character debate between historical figures on the core topic, with rebuttals and an impartial moderator synthesis.

🧬 Unrunnable Protocol pending

Analyze the core concept through dense pseudo-code "Unrunnable Protocol" frameworks, audited against pattern worship and framework fundamentalism.

📦 Output Lenses

These lenses transform your article into finished deliverables in different formats and styles. Each produces a standalone artifact ready for distribution or presentation.

🎯 Persuasive Essay pending

Reframe as a compelling persuasive essay using rhetorical techniques to build a strong argument.

📖 Narrative pending

Dramatize the content into a compelling narrative with illustrations.

💬 Comic Book pending

Generate a comic book representation of the content.

🔧 Technical Tutorial pending

Precise, in-depth technical breakdown with definitions, step-by-step mechanisms, code snippets, and worked examples.

🌐 HTML Webpage pending

Generate a standalone HTML webpage representation of the article with proper styling and structure.

🔬 Batch Lenses

Select which lenses to run, then execute them all sequentially.
