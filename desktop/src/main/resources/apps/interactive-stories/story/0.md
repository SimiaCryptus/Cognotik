## The Last Signal

Kara Voss stands on the rusted observation deck of Station Halcyon, watching the dead planet Erebos-9 turn slowly beneath her boots. She is the station's last engineer, the only one who stayed behind when the evacuation order came three years ago. Everyone else believed the old world-engine buried beneath the planet's crust was silent forever. Kara knows better — she has heard it humming in her dreams.

Tonight, for the first time since the evacuation, the humming became a signal. Real, measurable, undeniable. A pulse of encoded light rising from the crust, aimed directly at her station. Someone — or something — down there wants to be found.

Her console flickers with the decoded fragment: coordinates to an ancient vault, and a single word repeated in an unknown script: *"Awaken."*

Kara's hand hovers over the comm switch. She could call it in to the distant Coalition fleet, let them decide. She could take the old shuttle down alone and investigate herself. Or she could ignore it entirely and finally leave this graveyard station for good, signal be damned.

The console hums again, softer this time, almost patient — as if waiting for her answer.
