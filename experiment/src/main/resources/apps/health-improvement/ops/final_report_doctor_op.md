  tone** throughout. Omit irrelevant patient data — include only information that directly supports or contextualizes
  the AI-estimated medical assessment. This document should read like a well-organized intake summary a physician can
  quickly review.

## Chief Complaint & Relevant History

- Presenting symptoms with onset, duration, severity, and progression
- Pertinent patient-reported history (medical, family, lifestyle) — only what is clinically relevant

## Differential Diagnosis (Ranked)

- List of candidate conditions ranked by likelihood, with brief rationale for each
- Clearly distinguish between confirmed suspicions and speculative possibilities

## Supporting Evidence

- Cite reputable sources where applicable
- Summarize relevant findings from web research and medical literature that support the differential
- Note concordance or discordance between patient presentation and literature findings

## Recommended Next Steps

- Suggested diagnostic tests, lab work, or imaging
- Specialist referrals if warranted
- Proposed clinical pathway or triage priority

- **This report is not a medical diagnosis and does not replace professional medical advice**
