* Based on the refined perspectives and patient data, develop a personalized lifestyle and wellness plan.
* Focus on actionable, sustainable changes in diet, physical activity, and habit formation.

## Physical Activity & Movement

- Recommend activity levels that respect the patient's current strength and any recent injury history.
- Suggest specific types of movement (e.g., low-impact, strength-building, or flexibility) based on their preferences
  and physical constraints.
- Include a "ramp-up" strategy to prevent re-injury if the patient is resuming a fitness regime.

## Nutrition & Substance Use

- Provide dietary suggestions that support the primary health goals.
- Address any reported substance use (e.g., alcohol, tobacco) with practical, non-judgmental reduction strategies if
  desired by the patient.

## Social & Environmental Adjustments

- Account for the patient's social support system (or lack thereof) and time constraints (e.g., parenting, work).
- Suggest ways to find community or intellectual engagement that align with the patient's specific interests and values.

## Implementation Strategy

- Note the urgency and confidence level for each recommendation.
- Acknowledge the patient's specific concerns and barriers to change.
- Ensure the plan integrates with the Inner Development Plan for a holistic approach.
