* Analyze the brainstormed causes from multiple medical perspectives:

- **Primary Care Physician**: Which causes are most statistically likely given the symptom profile?
- **Specialist (relevant to dominant symptoms)**: What nuanced or easily-missed diagnoses should be considered?
- **Emergency Medicine**: Are there any red flags or time-sensitive conditions?
- **Patient Advocate**: What concerns might the patient have, and what information would help them understand the
  situation?

* Highlight areas of agreement and disagreement across perspectives
* Identify key uncertainties and gaps in the available information
